Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my smart lock to the app? ▼
Open the app and navigate to the device setup screen. Make sure your smart lock is powered on and within Bluetooth range (approximately 10 meters). The app will automatically scan for nearby compatible devices. Once your lock appears, tap "Connect" to pair it with the app.
Can I control my lock when I'm away from home? ▼
The app communicates with your smart lock via Bluetooth, so you need to be within Bluetooth range for direct control. However, scheduled operations will execute automatically based on your configured times, even when you're away.
How do I set up automatic lock schedules? ▼
Go to the Access tab, then tap "Schedules" in the Quick Actions section. Here you can create new schedules by setting lock and unlock times, choosing which days to repeat, and enabling or disabling each schedule independently.
What should I do if my lock is not responding? ▼
First, check that your lock has sufficient battery power. Then, ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone and you are within range. Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, try removing the device from the app and re-pairing it.
Is my data secure? ▼
Yes. All data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers. Bluetooth communication with your smart lock uses encrypted protocols. We take your security and privacy seriously. Please review our Privacy Policy for more details.
How do I remove a device from the app? ▼
Go to the Settings tab, scroll down to the "Danger Zone" section, and tap "Remove Device." This will remove your smart lock and all associated data from the app. You can always re-pair your device later.

Troubleshooting Steps

1

Check Bluetooth

Ensure Bluetooth is turned on in your device settings and the app has Bluetooth permission.

2

Verify Lock Power

Make sure your smart lock has sufficient battery. Replace batteries if the level is critically low.

3

Move Closer

Bluetooth range is typically 10 meters. Move closer to your lock and try again.

4

Restart the App

Close the app completely and reopen it. This resolves most temporary connection issues.

5

Re-pair Device

If all else fails, remove the device from Settings and pair it again from the setup screen.
