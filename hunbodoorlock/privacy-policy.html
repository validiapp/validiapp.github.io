Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how HunboDoorLock collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our smart lock companion application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Device Information: Smart lock device identifiers, firmware version, battery status, and connection data transmitted via Bluetooth.
  • Usage Data: Lock/unlock events, access logs, schedule configurations, and scene settings stored locally on your device.
  • Bluetooth Data: We use Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to discover and communicate with compatible smart lock devices. Bluetooth scanning data is processed locally and not transmitted to external servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Enable remote control and monitoring of your smart door lock
  • Display access history and activity timelines
  • Execute scheduled lock/unlock operations
  • Provide security alerts and tamper notifications
  • Improve app performance and user experience

3. Data Storage

All lock-related data, including access logs, schedules, and scene configurations, is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit personal data to remote servers. Your lock control data remains under your control at all times.

4. Bluetooth Permissions

This app requires Bluetooth permission to discover and connect to your smart door lock. Bluetooth is used exclusively for device communication and is not used for location tracking or advertising purposes. You can revoke Bluetooth permission at any time through your device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

This application does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share your personal information with third parties.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted Bluetooth communication and secure local storage. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the application. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
