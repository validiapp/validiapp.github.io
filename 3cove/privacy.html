Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

IronTrace operates entirely offline. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

Data Storage

All data you enter into IronTrace — including forging sessions, material inventory, gallery items, and photos — is stored exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database. No data leaves your device at any time.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Device identifiers
  • Browsing or search history
  • Contact lists or calendar data

Photos & Camera Access

IronTrace may request access to your device's photo library to allow you to attach process photos to gallery items. These photos are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted to any server.

PDF Export & Sharing

When you export data as PDF documents, the files are generated locally on your device. Sharing is handled through the native iOS share sheet. We have no access to or control over files once they are shared via third-party services you choose.

Third-Party Services

IronTrace does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app functions entirely without an internet connection.

Data Deletion

You can delete all data at any time through the Settings screen within the app. Uninstalling the app will also remove all locally stored data permanently.

Children's Privacy

IronTrace is a general-purpose tool for bladesmithing enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age, as we do not collect any personal information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Since IronTrace does not collect user data, we cannot notify you of changes directly — please review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Technical Support page.
