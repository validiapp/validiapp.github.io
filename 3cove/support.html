Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new forging session?

Navigate to the Trace tab and tap the "New Session" button. Fill in your steel type, temperature, technique, and other parameters, then tap Save.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. Nothing is uploaded to external servers. Your data stays private and accessible only to you.

How do I add photos to the gallery?

Go to the Gallery tab, tap "Add Work", and use the photo picker to select images from your device's photo library. You can add multiple photos per blade entry.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Go to Settings and choose from Export Sessions PDF, Export Materials PDF, or Export Gallery PDF. Files are generated locally and shared through the iOS share sheet.

How do I track material consumption?

In the Materials tab, each material entry includes total quantity and used quantity fields. The Usage tab shows visual breakdowns of consumption across all materials.

How do I delete data?

Individual items can be deleted by swiping left on list entries. To clear all data, go to Settings → Clear All Data. Note: this action is irreversible.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. IronTrace functions entirely offline. The only time network access is used is when you tap on the Privacy Policy or Support links, which open in your browser.

What iOS version is required?

IronTrace requires iOS 12.0 or later.
