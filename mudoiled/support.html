Quick Start Guides

📡

Connecting Your Device

Open the app, tap the + button, and allow Bluetooth access. Your device will appear in the scanner automatically.

💡

Adjusting Brightness

Use the brightness slider on the Home tab. Drag to any level between 1% and 100%, or use quick presets.

🎨

Changing Color Temperature

Select between Warm (2700K), Neutral White (4000K), or Cool (6500K) from the temperature section.

⏰

Setting Schedules

Go to Settings, find Schedules, and tap + to create an automated lighting schedule with repeat options.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Why can't my device be found?

Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone and the MudoiLed device is powered on and within range (approximately 10 meters). Try closing and reopening the Add Device screen to restart the scan.

❓ How do I reset my light to factory settings?

Turn the light off and on 5 times rapidly (1 second intervals). The light will blink 3 times to confirm the reset. Then re-add the device in the app.

❓ Can I control multiple lights at once?

Yes. Use the Scenes feature to apply brightness and color temperature settings across all connected devices simultaneously.

❓ Does the app work without an internet connection?

Yes. MudoiLed communicates directly with your devices via Bluetooth. No internet connection or cloud account is required.

❓ How accurate is the energy consumption data?

Energy data is estimated based on the device's rated wattage, brightness level, and active duration. It provides a close approximation for tracking purposes.

❓ What iOS version is required?

MudoiLed requires iOS 13.0 or later. It is compatible with iPhone models that support iOS 13 and above.

❓ Is my data shared with anyone?

No. All data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal or usage data. Please see our Privacy Policy for full details.
