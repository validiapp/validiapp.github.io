Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Overview

MudoiLed ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data remains on your device.

Information We Collect

Device Data (Stored Locally)

The App stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Device names and room assignments you configure
  • Lighting preferences (brightness levels, color temperature settings)
  • Custom scenes and schedules you create
  • Energy consumption data generated by the App

Bluetooth Information

The App uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to discover and communicate with compatible LED hardware devices. Bluetooth data is processed locally and is not transmitted to any server.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No personal identification information (name, email, phone)
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No data shared with third parties

Data Storage

All application data is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms (UserDefaults). No data is transmitted to external servers or cloud services.

If you delete the App, all stored data will be permanently removed from your device.

Bluetooth Permissions

The App requests Bluetooth permission solely for the purpose of discovering and communicating with MudoiLed hardware devices. This permission is required for the core functionality of the App.

You can revoke Bluetooth permission at any time through your device's Settings app. Without Bluetooth access, the App will not be able to connect to or control LED devices.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. We use open-source libraries for UI components only, which do not collect or transmit user data.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The App is a general-purpose utility that does not target or collect data from any specific age group.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
