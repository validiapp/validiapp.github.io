Overview

Smart LED Controller ("the App") is a smart lighting control application developed for iOS. This Privacy Policy describes how the App handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

📊

Information We Collect

The App is designed to minimize data collection. Here is a summary of the data handled by the App:

Data Type Purpose Storage
Device Names Identify your LED devices 📱 Local Only
Device Settings Brightness, color, mode preferences 📱 Local Only
Energy Records Track electricity usage & cost 📱 Local Only
Scene Configurations Custom lighting scenes 📱 Local Only
Electricity Rate Calculate energy costs 📱 Local Only
Bluetooth Data Communicate with LED hardware ⚡ Runtime Only
🔗

Bluetooth Usage

The App uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to communicate with compatible smart LED devices. Specifically:

  • Bluetooth is used solely for discovering, pairing, and controlling LED devices.
  • The App does not use Bluetooth to collect location data or track your movements.
  • Bluetooth data (device identifiers, signal strength) is used only during active communication and is not stored persistently.
  • You can revoke Bluetooth permission at any time in iOS Settings.
💾

Data Storage & Security

All user data is stored exclusively on your device using iOS-native storage mechanisms (UserDefaults and local JSON files). We implement the following security measures:

  • No remote servers: The App does not connect to any cloud services or remote databases.
  • No network requests: The App does not make HTTP/HTTPS requests for data transmission.
  • iOS sandbox: All data is protected by the iOS application sandbox.
  • User control: You can delete all stored data at any time from Settings > "Delete All Data".
🚫

What We Do NOT Collect

We want to be explicit about what we do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Analytics or usage metrics
  • Crash reports sent to external services
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Browsing history or search queries
  • Financial or payment information
🤝

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any user data to third parties. Since all data is stored locally on your device and we do not collect any personal information, there is no data to share.

The App does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or social media integrations.

👶

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App does not collect any personal information from any user, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.
