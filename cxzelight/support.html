Getting Started

Follow these simple steps to set up your smart LED lights.

2

3

4

Start Controlling

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

Which LED devices are compatible?

+
Smart LED Controller works with Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) enabled smart LED lights. Most mainstream BLE smart bulbs and LED strip controllers are supported. The app automatically detects compatible devices during the scanning process.

How do I connect a new device?

+
Open the app, tap the "+" button in the top-right corner of the Home screen. The app will start a radar scan for nearby Bluetooth devices. Once your LED device appears, tap it to complete pairing. Make sure Bluetooth is enabled and the LED device is powered on.

What are Scene Modes?

+
Scene Modes are pre-configured lighting presets designed for different activities. The app includes 8 built-in modes: Normal, Reading, Relax, Party, Sleep, Movie, Focus, and Romantic. Each mode adjusts brightness, color temperature, and power consumption to create the perfect atmosphere.

How does energy tracking work?

+
The app calculates energy consumption based on each device's wattage, brightness level, scene mode power multiplier, and usage duration. You can view energy trends by day, week, or month in the Billing tab. Set your local electricity rate in Settings to see estimated costs.

Can I set an auto-off timer?

+
Yes. Open any device's detail page and toggle the "Auto Off Timer" option. You can choose from preset durations: 15, 30, 60, or 120 minutes. The device will automatically turn off after the selected time elapses.

How do I change the LED color?

+
Navigate to the device detail page by tapping on a device card. You'll find a color picker wheel that allows you to select any color from the full RGB spectrum. The LED will update in real-time as you move your finger across the color wheel.

The app can't find my device. What should I do?

+
Please try these steps: (1) Ensure the LED device is powered on and within Bluetooth range. (2) Check that Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone/iPad. (3) Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth and make sure Smart LED Controller has permission. (4) Try turning Bluetooth off and on again. (5) Restart the LED device. (6) Force-close and reopen the app.

How do I delete all my data?

+
Go to Settings > Data > "Delete All Data". This will permanently remove all registered devices, energy records, and reset the app to its default state. This action cannot be undone. Your data is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any server.
