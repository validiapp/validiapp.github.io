Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a training session?

Open the Range tab, set your target distance and type, then tap "Start Training". Use the Hit and Miss buttons to record each throw.

How do I export my training data?

Go to Profile > Data section and tap "Export Data (PDF)". A professional report will be generated and you can share it through any app on your device.

Is my data backed up?

All data is stored locally on your device. We recommend using the PDF export feature periodically to keep a backup of your records.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Go to Profile > Data and tap "Clear All Data". You can also swipe left on individual records in the Records tab to delete them.

Does the app require internet?

No. RopeMark works completely offline. No internet connection is needed for any feature.

How are streaks calculated?

A streak is the number of consecutive successful throws during a session. Your best streak is saved as part of each session record.
