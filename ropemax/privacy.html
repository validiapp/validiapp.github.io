Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Summary: RopeMark does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your training data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

RopeMark does not collect any personal information. The app operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection, user account, or registration.

2. Data Storage

All data created within the app is stored locally on your device using a local database. This includes:

  • Training session records (throw counts, hit rates, distances)
  • Personal settings and preferences
  • Notes you add to training sessions

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it using the PDF export feature.

3. Data Sharing

RopeMark does not share any data with third parties. When you use the export feature, the PDF is generated locally and shared through your device's native share sheet — we have no access to exported files.

4. Third-Party Services

RopeMark does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no SDKs that collect usage data.

5. Children's Privacy

RopeMark does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through the app's Profile settings by using the "Clear All Data" option. Uninstalling the app will also remove all stored data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our support page.
