BoltNest - Hardware Storage Manager

We're here to help you get the most out of BoltNest. Below you'll find answers to common questions and guidance on using the app.

Getting Started

BoltNest is designed to help you manage hardware inventory with ease. Here's how to get started:

  • Workbench: Your main dashboard showing inventory overview, quick search, and low-stock alerts.
  • Shelves: Organize items by zone (A/B), manage categories, and record precise shelf locations.
  • Records: Track all stock-in and stock-out operations, and maintain audit history.
  • Settings: Configure alerts, manage data, and access support resources.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new inventory item?

Navigate to the Shelves tab and tap the "+" button in the top right corner. Fill in the item details including name, SKU, category, quantity, and shelf location.

How do I record stock movement?

Go to the Records tab and tap the "+" button to create a new record. Select the item, choose stock-in or stock-out, enter the quantity, and add an optional note.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to external servers. Your inventory information stays completely private and under your control.

How do I reset my data?

Go to Settings → Data Management → Reset All Data. Please note this action cannot be undone and will permanently delete all inventory items and records.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, BoltNest works entirely offline. No internet connection is required for any functionality.

How do I search for items?

Use the Quick Search on the Workbench tab. You can search by item name, SKU code, or category name.
