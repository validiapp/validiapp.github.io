Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Overview

ReallyNight ("the App") is designed with your privacy as a priority. We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly how your information is handled when using the App.

Information We Do Not Collect

ReallyNight does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or behavioral tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No third-party analytics SDKs

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only, using standard iOS storage mechanisms (UserDefaults):

  • Device configuration: Your connected light device settings including brightness, color mode, and on/off state.
  • Schedule data: Any automated on/off schedules you create.
  • Energy statistics: Estimated energy consumption records generated locally.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Bluetooth Usage

ReallyNight uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to discover and communicate with compatible smart light devices. Bluetooth is used solely for:

  • Scanning for nearby ReallyNight-compatible devices
  • Establishing a connection with your smart light
  • Sending control commands (on/off, brightness, color mode)

No Bluetooth data is recorded, logged, or transmitted beyond the direct device-to-device communication.

Notifications

The App may request permission to send local notifications for scheduled light events. These notifications are generated entirely on-device and do not involve any remote push notification services.

Third-Party Services

ReallyNight does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The App functions entirely offline after initial download from the App Store.

Data Deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time through the Settings page within the App by using the "Reset All Data" option. Alternatively, uninstalling the App will remove all associated data from your device.

Children's Privacy

ReallyNight does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the App is compliant with COPPA and similar regulations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
