Technical Support

Find answers to common questions and get help with your ReallyNight smart light.

Getting Started

How do I connect my light?

Open the ReallyNight app and tap the "+" button on the Home screen. Make sure your light is powered on and in pairing mode. The app will automatically scan for nearby devices using Bluetooth. When your light appears, tap "Connect" to pair it.

What permissions does the app need?

ReallyNight requires only Bluetooth permission to discover and communicate with your smart light. No other permissions (location, camera, microphone, etc.) are needed. You can manage permissions in your device's Settings app.

Which devices are compatible?

ReallyNight works with all ReallyNight-branded smart light devices that support Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). The app requires iOS 13.0 or later on iPhone.

Features & Usage

How do I adjust brightness?

On the Home screen, use the brightness slider to adjust from 1% to 100%. The change is applied to your light in real time. The default brightness when turning on is 100%.

What color modes are available?

ReallyNight offers three color temperature modes:

  • Cool: A bluish-white tone ideal for focus and productivity.
  • Neutral White: A balanced warm-white for everyday use.
  • Warm: A golden tone perfect for relaxation and evening use.
How do schedules work?

Navigate to the On/Off tab to create schedules. Set a turn-on time and turn-off time, then choose which days to repeat. You can create multiple schedules and toggle them on or off individually. The app will send local notifications when a scheduled event occurs.

How is energy usage calculated?

Energy statistics are estimated based on your light's usage duration and brightness level. View daily, weekly, and monthly consumption trends on the Home screen or in the Energy Details view. All calculations happen locally on your device.

Troubleshooting

My light won't connect.

Try the following steps:

  • Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone (Settings > Bluetooth).
  • Make sure your light is powered on and within range (typically 10 meters).
  • Restart the app and try scanning again.
  • Power cycle your light (turn off, wait 10 seconds, turn back on).
  • If the issue persists, try restarting your iPhone.
Schedules are not triggering.

Ensure notification permissions are granted for ReallyNight in your device Settings. Also verify that the schedule is toggled on (the switch should be orange). Note that the app needs to be running in the background for Bluetooth commands to be sent.

How do I reset the app?

Go to Settings > Data Management > Reset All Data. This will clear all saved device information, schedules, and preferences. You can also uninstall and reinstall the app for a complete reset.
