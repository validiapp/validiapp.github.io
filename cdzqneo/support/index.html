Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a training session?

Open the Balance tab, select your training mode (Single Leg, Eyes Closed, Left Foot, or Right Foot), then tap "Start Training" to begin the timer. Tap "Stop" when finished, and save your session with optional notes.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. Your training history, journal entries, and progress are completely private.

How does the streak system work?

Complete at least one training session per day to maintain your streak. If you miss a day, your current streak resets to zero, but your longest streak record is preserved.

Can I export my data?

Yes, go to Settings > Data > Export Data to generate a text summary of all your sessions and progress that you can share or save.

How do I reset all data?

Navigate to Settings > Data > Clear All Data. This will permanently delete all sessions, journal entries, and progress. This action cannot be undone.

What is the symmetry score?

The symmetry score measures how balanced your left and right foot performance is. A score of 100% means perfect balance between both sides.

Training Tips

  • Start with short sessions and gradually increase duration
  • Practice on both sides equally for better symmetry
  • Use a fixed gaze point to improve stability
  • Train daily for best results - consistency matters most
  • Record notes after each session to track what works
