Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

CDZQneo is designed with your privacy as a priority. This app operates entirely offline and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data Collection

CDZQneo does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No personal identifiers are collected
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No location data
  • No network requests or internet connectivity required
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs

Data Storage

All app data (training sessions, journal entries, settings, and progress) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Data Sharing

CDZQneo does not share any data with third parties. Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing to share.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time through the Settings screen within the app. Uninstalling the app will also permanently remove all stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

CDZQneo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit our support page.
