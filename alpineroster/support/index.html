Support Center

Find answers to common questions and get help with AlpineRoster.

Quick Start Guide

👥

Add Partners

Go to the Roster tab and tap the + button. Choose a sport type and fill in your companion's details.

📍

Log Trips

Navigate to the Trips tab and tap + to record a new adventure with location, metrics, and notes.

🗺

Save Routes

Use the Routes tab to save peaks, dive sites, and cycling trails with difficulty and season info.

📤

Export Data

Go to Settings and tap "Export Data (PDF)" to generate and share a complete report of your records.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data stored in the cloud? ▼
No. All data is stored locally on your device using an on-device database. AlpineRoster does not connect to any servers or cloud services. Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to export and share it.
How do I export my data? ▼
Go to the Settings tab and tap "Export Data (PDF)". The app will generate a formatted PDF document containing your partners, trips, and routes. You can then share it using any method available on your device (email, AirDrop, messaging, etc.).
Can other people see my notes? ▼
No. The personal notes feature (accessible from each partner's profile) is completely private. Notes and images are stored locally on your device and are only visible within the app. They are not shared, synced, or transmitted in any way.
What happens to my data if I delete the app? ▼
If you uninstall AlpineRoster, all data stored by the app will be permanently deleted from your device. We recommend using the PDF export feature to create a backup before uninstalling.
Does the app require an internet connection? ▼
No. AlpineRoster works entirely offline. You do not need an internet connection to use any feature of the app. All data is stored and processed locally on your device.
What sports does the app support? ▼
AlpineRoster supports three outdoor sports: climbing (mountaineering, rock climbing), diving (scuba diving, freediving), and cycling (road, mountain, gravel). Each sport has tailored fields for tracking relevant metrics like elevation gain, dive depth, and ride distance.
How do I add images to partner notes? ▼
Open a partner's profile and tap "Notes" to enter the notes screen. Tap the image icon in the bottom-left corner of the input bar to select an image from your device's photo library. The image will be saved locally alongside your text notes.
