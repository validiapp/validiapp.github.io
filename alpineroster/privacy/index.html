Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

In short: AlpineRoster does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

Introduction

AlpineRoster ("the App") is a personal utility tool designed for outdoor enthusiasts to organize their companion profiles, log trips, and save route information. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

AlpineRoster does not collect any personal data. The App operates entirely offline and does not communicate with any external servers, APIs, or cloud services.

Data Storage

All data you enter into the App — including partner profiles, trip logs, route records, and personal notes — is stored locally on your device using an on-device database. This data is:

  • Stored exclusively on your device's local storage
  • Not transmitted to any server or third party
  • Not backed up to any cloud service by the App
  • Accessible only through the App on your device

Types of Information Stored Locally

The App allows you to store the following types of information on your device:

  • Companion names, contact details, and skill levels
  • Emergency contact information
  • Trip details including locations, dates, and activity metrics
  • Route information including locations and difficulty ratings
  • Personal notes and images associated with companions

Third-Party Services

AlpineRoster does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App does not use cookies, beacons, or similar tracking technologies.

Data Export

The App provides a PDF export feature that generates a document on your device. This document is shared using your device's native sharing capabilities. AlpineRoster does not control or monitor how you choose to share the exported file.

Data Deletion

You can delete any individual record (partner, trip, route, or note) directly within the App. Uninstalling the App from your device will permanently remove all data stored by the App.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children. The App stores only the data that users voluntarily enter.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Support page.
