Frequently Asked Questions

Q How do I add a new beetle record?

Navigate to the Reserve tab and tap the "+" button in the top right corner. Fill in the species name, select the life stage and gender, add measurements, and save your record.

Q Can I track both income and expenses?

Yes. In the Balance tab, tap "+" to create a new transaction. Choose between Income or Expense, select a category (such as Feeding, Sales, or Equipment), enter the amount, and save.

Q Is my data stored online?

No. Reelora is a fully offline application. All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your information on external servers.

Q How do I clear all my data?

Go to Settings and tap "Clear All Data." This will permanently delete all beetle records, transactions, and tasks. Please note this action cannot be undone.

Q What beetle species can I track?

You can track any beetle species. Simply type the species name when creating a new record. Common species include Dynastes hercules, Allomyrina dichotoma, Megasoma elephas, and Lucanus cervus.

Q How do I set task reminders?

In the Tackle tab, tap "+" to create a task. Set a title, choose a priority level (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent), select a due date, and save. Tasks will appear organized by their due dates.
