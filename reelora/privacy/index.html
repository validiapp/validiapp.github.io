Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Our Commitment

Reelora is designed with your privacy as a core principle. We believe your beetle breeding records, financial data, and task management information are personal and should remain entirely under your control.

Reelora operates entirely offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data with any third party.

Information We Do Not Collect

Reelora does not collect any personal information whatsoever. Specifically:

  • We do not require account registration or login credentials
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • We do not access your device's camera, microphone, contacts, or location
  • We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks
  • We do not collect device identifiers or usage statistics
  • We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies

Data Storage

All data you enter into Reelora — including beetle records, financial transactions, and tasks — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage mechanisms provided by iOS.

This data is never transmitted over the internet. It remains on your device at all times and is accessible only through the Reelora app.

If you uninstall Reelora, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted from your device.

Third-Party Services

Reelora does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The app functions entirely without an internet connection.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data is protected by your device's built-in security features, including:

  • iOS device encryption
  • Passcode and biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID)
  • App sandboxing enforced by iOS

Children's Privacy

Reelora does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect any data, no special provisions for children's data are necessary.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Reelora's data practices, please visit our Technical Support page.
