🔧 Getting Started

Enable Bluetooth

Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone in Settings > Bluetooth.

Power On Your Fan

Press the power button on your JubacCool smart fan to enter pairing mode.

Open the App

Launch JubacCool app and tap the "+" button on the Home screen.

Grant Permission

Allow Bluetooth access when prompted to enable device discovery.

Connect Device

Wait for your fan to appear in the scan results and tap "Connect".

Start Using

You're all set! Control your fan from the Home tab.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't my app find the fan?

Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone, and your fan is in pairing mode. Try moving closer to the fan (within 10 meters) and restarting both the app and the fan.

How do I reset my fan?

Press and hold the power button on your fan for 10 seconds until the LED blinks rapidly. Then try reconnecting via the app.

Can I control multiple fans?

Currently, the app supports connecting to one fan at a time. Disconnect from the current fan before connecting to another.

Does the timer work when the app is closed?

Yes, once a timer is set, the command is sent to the fan's firmware. The timer operates independently of the app.

What iOS version is required?

JubacCool requires iOS 13.0 or later and supports all iPhones with Bluetooth 4.0+.

Is my data stored on a server?

No. All your usage data and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit any personal information.
