Getting Started

System Requirements

  • iOS 13.0 or later
  • iPhone with Bluetooth 4.0+ capability
  • Compatible smart lock device

Initial Setup

  1. Open iDoorHudon and navigate to the Door tab
  2. Tap the + button to begin device discovery
  3. Grant Bluetooth permission when prompted
  4. Ensure your smart lock is in pairing mode (refer to lock manual)
  5. Select your device from the list and follow on-screen instructions

Bluetooth Connectivity

iDoorHudon uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to communicate with your smart lock. Ensure Bluetooth is enabled in your device settings and that you are within range (typically 10-15 meters) of your lock.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: My lock is not appearing during scan?

Ensure your lock is in pairing mode, Bluetooth is enabled, and you are within range. Try restarting both the app and your lock device.

Q: How secure is the connection?

All communications between the app and your lock use AES-256 encryption. No lock credentials are stored on remote servers.

Q: Can I share access with family members?

Yes, use the Guest Access scene or share temporary access through the app's sharing features.

Q: What happens if my phone battery dies?

Your smart lock continues to operate normally. You can still use physical keys or keypad (if equipped) to access your lock.

Q: How do I update the lock firmware?

Navigate to Settings > Firmware Update. The app will check for available updates and guide you through the process.
