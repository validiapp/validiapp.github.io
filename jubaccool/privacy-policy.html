Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

JubacCool Smart Fan

📋 Introduction

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the JubacCool Smart Fan application.

📱 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Bluetooth Data: Device identifiers required to establish and maintain connection with your smart fan.
  • Usage Data: Fan operation history including usage duration, speed settings, and scheduling preferences stored locally on your device.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and app version for compatibility purposes.

🔐 How We Use Your Information

  • To enable Bluetooth connectivity between your phone and smart fan device.
  • To store your preferences and settings locally for a personalized experience.
  • To display usage statistics and analytics within the app.
  • To improve app performance and user experience.

💾 Data Storage

All your personal data and usage statistics are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your personal information to any external servers. Your fan control settings and usage history remain private and under your control.

🤝 Third-Party Services

This application does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share, sell, or distribute your personal data to any third parties.

🔒 Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data. Bluetooth communication uses standard iOS security protocols. All data stored on your device is protected by your device's built-in security features.

👶 Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

✏️ Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
