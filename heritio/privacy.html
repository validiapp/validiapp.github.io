Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our Commitment: Heritio is designed with your privacy as a priority. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

Information We Collect

Heritio does not collect any personal information. All data you create within the app — including building records, observations, preferences, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage.

Data Storage

All user-generated content is saved locally on your device. We do not use cloud services, remote databases, or any form of network transmission to handle your data. This means:

  • Your building observations remain entirely on your device.
  • No account registration is required.
  • No data is uploaded to any server.
  • Deleting the app will remove all associated data.

Third-Party Services

Heritio does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share your data with any external parties.

Network Usage

The app functions entirely offline. No internet connection is required to use any feature of Heritio. The app does not make any network requests.

Children's Privacy

Heritio does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The app does not require any personal information to function.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through the Settings page within the app. Alternatively, uninstalling the app will permanently remove all stored data from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
