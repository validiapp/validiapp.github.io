Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new building observation?

Tap the "+" button on the Discover tab. You'll be guided through a three-step process to record the building name, architectural details, and your personal observations.

What architectural styles can I choose from?

Heritio supports eight common architectural styles: Gothic, Baroque, Art Deco, Victorian, Colonial, Modernist, Romanesque, and Traditional. Each comes with its own visual identity.

How do I change the color theme?

Go to Settings and scroll to the Appearance section. Choose from themes like Sandstone, Slate, Terracotta, Moss, Cobalt, or Dusk.

Is my data backed up?

All data is stored locally on your device. We recommend keeping regular device backups to ensure your observations are safe.

How do I delete a record?

Long-press on any building card in the Discover tab, then select "Delete." You can also delete from the detail view of any record.

Does the app need internet access?

No. Heritio works entirely offline. All features are available without an internet connection.

What happens when I clear all data?

Going to Settings > Clear All Data permanently removes all building records and preferences. This cannot be undone.
