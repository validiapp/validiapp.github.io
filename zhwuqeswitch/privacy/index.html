Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

Your privacy matters. Smart Switch is designed with privacy at its core. We collect minimal data and never sell your personal information to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

Smart Switch collects only the minimum information necessary to provide our services:

  • Device Information: We store your smart switch device identifiers, names, and configurations locally on your device to enable remote control functionality.
  • Bluetooth Data: The app accesses Bluetooth to discover and communicate with compatible smart switch devices. Bluetooth scan results are processed locally and not transmitted to external servers.
  • Usage Data: Device usage statistics (on/off times, power consumption) are stored locally on your device for analytics display purposes.
  • Schedule Data: Timer schedules and scene configurations are stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively for:

  • Enabling remote control of your smart switch devices
  • Managing timer schedules and scene automations
  • Displaying usage analytics and power consumption data
  • Improving app performance and user experience

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your iOS device. We do not maintain external servers or cloud databases that store your personal information. Your data remains on your device and under your control at all times.

4. Third-Party Services

Smart Switch does not integrate with third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or data brokers. We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third parties.

5. Bluetooth Permissions

The app requires Bluetooth access to discover and communicate with smart switch devices. This permission is requested when you first attempt to add a new device. You can revoke this permission at any time through your device's Settings app.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Local-only data storage with iOS built-in encryption
  • Secure Bluetooth communication protocols
  • No external data transmission

7. Children's Privacy

Smart Switch does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The app is designed for general audiences and does not contain content directed at children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through our Support page.
