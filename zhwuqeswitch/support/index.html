Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect a new smart switch device?
Open the app, tap the "+" button on the Home screen. The app will request Bluetooth permission if not already granted. Once Bluetooth is enabled, the app will scan for nearby smart switch devices. When your device appears in the list, tap "Connect" to pair it.
Why can't my app find my smart switch?
Make sure your smart switch is powered on and within Bluetooth range (typically 10 meters). Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone in Settings. Try restarting both the app and your smart switch device. If the problem persists, try resetting the smart switch according to the manufacturer's instructions.
How do I set up a timer schedule?
Navigate to the "Time" tab, then tap the "+" button. Select the desired time using the time picker, choose whether to turn the switch ON or OFF, select repeat days if needed, and tap "Save Schedule". Your schedule will be automatically executed at the specified times.
What are Scenes and how do I use them?
Scenes are pre-configured automation presets that control one or more devices with a single tap. For example, a "Good Night" scene can turn off all lights simultaneously. Navigate to the "Time" tab, switch to the "Scenes" segment, and tap any scene to activate it.
Does the app work without an internet connection?
Yes! Smart Switch communicates with your devices directly via Bluetooth. No internet connection or cloud service is required for basic on/off control and scheduling. All data is stored locally on your device.
How do I view my power usage statistics?
On the Home screen, scroll down to the "Usage Overview" chart. You can toggle between weekly and monthly views using the segment control. The chart displays your daily device usage in hours, helping you understand your energy consumption patterns.

Troubleshooting Guide

1

Check Bluetooth

Go to Settings > Bluetooth and ensure it is turned on. Also verify that Smart Switch has Bluetooth permission in Settings > Privacy > Bluetooth.

2

Restart the App

Force close the Smart Switch app by swiping it up in the app switcher, then reopen it. This resolves most connectivity issues.

3

Power Cycle Your Device

Turn your smart switch off and on again. Wait 10 seconds before attempting to reconnect from the app.

4

Update the App

Make sure you are running the latest version of Smart Switch. Check the App Store for available updates.
