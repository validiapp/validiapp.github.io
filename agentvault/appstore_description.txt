English (150 words):

A lightweight offline utility designed for professionals who manage multiple automated workflows and digital processes. Effortlessly create detailed records for each task, track execution history with timestamped activity logs, and gain valuable insights through interactive visual analytics including performance charts, status distribution, and trend analysis. The built-in calendar view helps you review past activities by date, while smart search and filtering options make it easy to locate specific records instantly. Organize entries with custom categories, tags, and status indicators to maintain a clear overview of all your processes. Every piece of data stays exclusively on your device with no account registration, no cloud storage, and no data collection of any kind. The clean, intuitive interface is built for daily use, helping you stay organized and make informed decisions about your workflows without compromising your privacy.
