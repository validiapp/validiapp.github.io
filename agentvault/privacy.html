Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Overview

AgentVault is designed with your privacy as a core principle. This application operates entirely offline and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter into the app remains exclusively on your device.

Data Collection

AgentVault does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or fingerprints
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Cookies or tracking technologies

The app requires no internet connection to function. All features work completely offline.

Data Storage

All data entered into AgentVault is stored locally on your device using the iOS standard data persistence mechanisms. This data includes:

  • Workflow records and configurations you create
  • Activity logs and execution history
  • Your app preferences and settings

This data is never transmitted to any server, third-party service, or external system. It resides solely within the application's sandboxed storage on your iOS device.

Third-Party Services

AgentVault does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data processing services. No SDKs or frameworks that collect user data are included in the application.

Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Use the "Clear All Data" option in Settings to permanently remove all stored information
  • Uninstalling the app will remove all associated data from your device
  • Individual records can be deleted through the standard app interface

Children's Privacy

AgentVault is a general-purpose utility application. It does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users, as it collects no data whatsoever.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Support page for contact information.
