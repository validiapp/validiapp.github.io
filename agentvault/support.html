Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new workflow record? ▼

Navigate to the Agents tab and tap the "+" button in the upper-right corner. Fill in the details including name, category, platform, and description, then tap Save.

Is my data backed up to the cloud? ▼

No. All data is stored locally on your device. The app works entirely offline and does not connect to any server. If you delete the app, all data will be permanently removed.

How can I delete my data? ▼

Go to Settings and select "Clear All Data" to remove all records at once. You can also swipe left on individual records to delete them one by one.

What do the analytics charts show? ▼

The Analytics tab provides visual summaries including daily activity trends, status distribution pie charts, success rate comparisons, and top-performing workflow rankings.

Can I filter activities by date? ▼

Yes. The Activity tab includes a calendar view. Tap any date to filter events to that specific day. You can also filter by event type using the segment control.

Does the app require an internet connection? ▼

No. The app is designed to function completely offline. No internet connection is required for any feature.
