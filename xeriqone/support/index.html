Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up your beekeeping operation in Nectarix:

  1. Open the Apiary tab and create your first apiary location
  2. Switch to Colonies and add your hives to the apiary
  3. Swipe left on a colony card to record an inspection
  4. Go to Nectar to add flora sources and log honey harvests
  5. Use Seasons to plan and track seasonal activities

Tips & Tricks

Quick Delete Swipe left on any card to reveal the delete action. Confirm to permanently remove the record.
Keyboard Dismiss Tap the "Done" button above the keyboard to dismiss it, or tap anywhere outside the text field.
PDF Export Go to Settings → Export Data to generate a comprehensive PDF report of all your records, then share via AirDrop, email, or save to Files.
Offline Mode Nectarix works entirely offline. No internet connection is ever required. Your data is always accessible.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my data stored?
All data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Nothing is ever sent to external servers. Your data is as secure as your device itself.
Can I backup my data?
Yes. Use the Export Data feature in Settings to generate a PDF report. You can also rely on your device's built-in backup through iCloud device backup, which includes app data.
What happens if I delete the app?
All data stored within the app will be permanently removed. We strongly recommend exporting a PDF report before uninstalling.
Does the app need internet access?
No. Nectarix is designed to work completely offline. All features function without any internet connection.
How do I record an inspection?
Go to the Colonies tab, then either tap the Inspections sub-tab and use the + button, or swipe left on a colony card and tap "Inspect" to create an inspection linked to that specific colony.
Can I track multiple apiaries?
Absolutely. You can create unlimited apiary locations, each with their own set of colonies. The dashboard shows aggregated statistics across all your apiaries.

Troubleshooting

  • App feels slow: Try closing and reopening the app. Performance may be affected with very large datasets.
  • Data appears missing: Ensure you haven't accidentally deleted records. Check if you're viewing the correct filter/tab.
  • PDF export fails: Ensure you have sufficient storage space on your device. Try restarting the app.
  • Keyboard won't dismiss: Tap the "Done" button above the keyboard or tap outside the text field.
