Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

No data collection. No tracking. No ads. Your data stays on your device.

1. Introduction

Nectarix ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our beekeeping management application.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Nectarix does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data you enter into the App is stored exclusively on your local device.

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No third-party advertising SDKs
  • No cookies or web beacons
  • No location data transmitted externally
  • No social media integrations

3. Data Storage

All beekeeping records are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. This includes:

  • Apiary locations and names
  • Colony/hive information and status records
  • Inspection logs and observations
  • Honey harvest records with quality metrics
  • Nectar source catalog entries
  • Seasonal planning activities
  • User preferences and settings

This data is never transmitted to any server and remains entirely under your control.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. The only mechanism by which data can leave your device is through the PDF export feature, which:

  • You explicitly initiate by tapping "Export Data"
  • Uses the native iOS share sheet
  • You control the destination (email, AirDrop, files, etc.)
  • Contains only the data you choose to export

5. Data Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features including passcode lock, Face ID / Touch ID, and hardware encryption. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device passcode enabled
  • Keeping iOS updated to the latest version
  • Periodically exporting PDF reports as backup

6. Children's Privacy

Nectarix is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. The App is designed for adult beekeepers managing their operations.

7. Third-Party Services

Nectarix does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. The app operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection for any functionality.

8. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Individual records can be deleted by swiping left within the app
  • All data can be cleared from Settings > Clear All Data
  • Uninstalling the app removes all associated data permanently

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device and we have no access to it, you inherently have full control including the right to access, modify, export, and delete all your data at any time.
