Astrev

A thoughtful deep sky observation logging tool for amateur astronomers who appreciate precision and simplicity.

🔭 Observation Sessions

Start timed observation sessions with real-time recording. Track seeing conditions, transparency, and magnification for each target.

📖 Complete Records

Browse your observation history with detailed notes, conditions data, and session duration. Export everything as a formatted PDF.

⭐ Deep Sky Catalog

Built-in catalog of popular deep sky objects including Messier and NGC targets with coordinates, magnitude, and descriptions.

🔧 Equipment Manager

Organize your telescopes, eyepieces, filters, cameras, and accessories in one place with full specifications tracking.

📊 Session Analytics

View statistics on total observation hours, average seeing conditions, and most-observed targets across all your sessions.

🔒 Offline & Private

All data stored locally on your device. No account required, no cloud sync, no tracking. Your observations stay yours.
