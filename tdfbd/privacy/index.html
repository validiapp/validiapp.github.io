Privacy Policy

Astrev - Deep Sky Observation Log

Overview

Astrev is designed with your privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All information you enter into the app remains exclusively on your device.

Data Collection

Astrev does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Device identifiers
  • Browsing or search history
  • Contact information

Data Storage

All observation records, equipment data, target catalogs, and preferences are stored locally on your device using a local database. No data is synced to any cloud service or external server.

When you delete data within the app or uninstall the app, all associated data is permanently removed from your device.

Data Sharing

The app includes an export feature that generates a PDF document of your observation records. This file is created locally and shared only through your device's native sharing interface. We do not have access to exported files.

Third-Party Services

Astrev does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app functions entirely offline without requiring an internet connection.

Children's Privacy

Astrev does not knowingly collect information from children. The app does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the app's data practices, please visit our Technical Support page.
