Astrev - Deep Sky Observation Log

Getting Started

Astrev is a deep sky observation logging tool designed for amateur astronomers. Here's how to get started:

Observe

Starting an Observation Session

Navigate to the Observe tab, enter your target name (e.g., M31, NGC 7000), select the object type, set your seeing and transparency conditions, then tap "Start Observation" to begin timing your session.

Records

Viewing Past Observations

The Records tab shows all your completed observation sessions. You can sort by date, name, or seeing conditions. Swipe left on any record to delete it.

Export

Exporting Your Data

Tap the PDF icon in the Records tab or go to Settings > Export Data to generate a formatted PDF of all your observations. The file is shared through your device's native share sheet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Astrev works entirely offline. All data is stored locally on your device.

How do I add my equipment?

Go to the Gear tab and tap the + button. Select the equipment type (telescope, eyepiece, filter, camera, mount, or accessory) and fill in the details.

Can I recover deleted data?

No. Once data is deleted, it cannot be recovered. We recommend exporting your observations regularly as a backup.

What deep sky targets are included?

The built-in catalog includes popular Messier objects and select NGC targets. You can search, filter by type, and mark favorites for quick access.

How is the seeing condition scale defined?

Seeing is rated on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 represents poor atmospheric stability and 5 represents excellent, steady conditions ideal for high-magnification viewing.

System Requirements

Astrev requires iOS 12.0 or later. The app is compatible with iPhone and iPad.
