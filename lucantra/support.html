Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure?

All data is stored locally on your device. Lucantra does not connect to the internet and does not transmit any data. Your financial and breeding records never leave your phone.

How do I add a new transaction?

Navigate to the Accounts tab and tap the "+" button in the upper right corner. Choose between Income and Expense, enter the details, select a category, and tap Save.

How do I record beetle specimen data?

Go to the Science tab and tap "+". Fill in the species, life stage, measurements, and environment data. You can track weight, length, temperature, humidity, and substrate type.

Can I filter transactions by date?

Yes. Tap the calendar icon on the Accounts tab to open the date picker. Days with recorded transactions are marked with a dot. Select any date to view transactions from that day.

How do I delete a record?

Long-press on any transaction or beetle record to reveal the context menu, then select "Delete". You can also delete records from the detail view.

How do I clear all data?

Go to Settings and tap "Clear Data". You will be asked to confirm before all transactions and beetle records are permanently deleted.

What categories are available for transactions?

Lucantra includes 11 categories designed for beetle breeding: Feeding, Substrate, Equipment, Breeding, Sales, Purchase, Veterinary, Shipping, Housing, Supplements, and Other.

Which beetle species are supported?

The app includes presets for Dynastes hercules, Lucanus cervus, Chalcosoma atlas, Megasoma mars, Allomyrina dichotoma, Prosopocoilus giraffa, Dorcus titanus, and a custom "Other" option.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Lucantra works entirely offline. You can use all features without any internet connection.

What iOS version is required?

Lucantra requires iOS 14.0 or later. It is compatible with iPhone and iPad.

Quick Start Guide

Open the Capital tab to see your financial overview with charts showing income and expense trends.
Go to Accounts to add your first transaction. Tap "+" and fill in the details.
Visit Science to track your beetle colonies. Add specimen records with species and stage data.
Check Settings to access support links, privacy policy, and data management options.
