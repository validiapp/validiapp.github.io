Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Lucantra is designed to work entirely offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Lucantra does not collect any personal information. The application operates completely offline and does not communicate with any external servers, APIs, or third-party services.

All data you enter into the app — including financial transactions, beetle breeding records, notes, and settings — is stored locally on your device using the iOS built-in storage mechanisms.

2. Data Storage

Your data is stored exclusively on your device. Specifically:

  • Transaction records are saved in your device's local storage.
  • Beetle specimen records remain on your device.
  • No data is uploaded to any cloud service or remote server.
  • No backup of your data is maintained by us on any external system.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with third parties. Since we do not collect any data, there is nothing to share.

4. Third-Party Services

Lucantra does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not contain any advertising or in-app tracking mechanisms.

5. Network Access

Lucantra does not require internet access to function. The application works entirely offline. No network requests are made during normal operation of the app.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time through the Settings screen within the app using the "Clear Data" option. Uninstalling the app from your device will also remove all associated data permanently.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect any personal data from any user, it is inherently safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device and we have no access to it, you have complete control over your data at all times. You can view, modify, or delete your data directly within the application.

Questions about privacy?

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@lucantra.app.
