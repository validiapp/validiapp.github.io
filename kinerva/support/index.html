Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Kinerva works completely offline. All your training data is stored locally on your device and never sent to any server.

How do I reset my training data?

Go to Settings and tap "Clear Data". This will permanently remove all your training records, progress statistics, and streak information.

What exercises are available?

The app includes 8 different exercise types: Finger Tapping, Pinch Grip, Finger Spread, Thumb Opposition, Wrist Rotation, Finger Sequence, Grip Strength, and Finger Flexion.

How is accuracy calculated?

Accuracy is measured based on your repetition rate relative to the session duration and difficulty level. Higher consistency results in better accuracy scores.

What are difficulty levels?

Each exercise can be performed at four levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Expert. Choose based on your current ability and progress gradually.

How do streaks work?

Complete at least one exercise session per day to maintain your streak. Missing a day resets your current streak, but your longest streak record is preserved.
