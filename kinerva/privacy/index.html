Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Kinerva is designed with your privacy in mind. All your data stays on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

Information We Collect

Kinerva does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. All training data, exercise records, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device using local storage mechanisms.

Data Storage

The following types of data are stored locally on your device:

  • Training session records (exercise type, duration, repetitions, accuracy)
  • Progress statistics and weekly goals
  • App preferences and settings
  • Daily streak information

This data is stored using iOS standard local storage (UserDefaults) and remains entirely on your device.

Internet Access

Kinerva does not require an internet connection to function. The app operates entirely offline and does not make any network requests for its core functionality.

Third-Party Services

Kinerva does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with third parties.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your training data at any time through the Settings section of the app. When you delete the app from your device, all associated data is permanently removed.

Children's Privacy

Kinerva does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The app is suitable for all ages and does not require any personal information to use.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
