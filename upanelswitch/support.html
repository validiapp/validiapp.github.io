Support

uPanelSwitch is a smart switch control app. We can help with connection issues, timer setup, and general questions.

Contact support

Email support@upanelswitch.com

We'll reply as soon as reasonably possible.

What we can help with

  • Pairing a new switch device via Bluetooth
  • Troubleshooting connection or signal issues
  • Setting up and editing on/off timer schedules
  • Assigning devices to rooms
  • General app questions and feedback

Getting started

  • Open uPanelSwitch and tap the + button
  • Grant Bluetooth permission when prompted
  • Ensure your switch device is powered on and in pairing mode
  • Tap "Connect" when the device appears
  • Your switch is ready to control

Frequently asked questions

My device doesn't appear in the scan list

Confirm your switch is in pairing mode and Bluetooth is enabled in Settings. Try moving closer to the device and re-opening the Add Device screen.

The switch doesn't respond to on/off commands

Wait for the 3-second confirmation cycle to complete. If it still fails, try disconnecting and reconnecting the device from the app.

How do I set up a timer schedule?

Open a device, tap "Edit" in the Timer Control section. Set your desired on and off times, choose repeat days, and save.

Can I control multiple switches?

Yes. Add each switch individually through the Add Device flow. All connected switches appear on the Home screen.

Is my data stored in the cloud?

No. All configuration data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all data permanently.

System requirements

  • iOS 13.0 or later
  • Bluetooth 4.0 (BLE) capable device
  • Compatible uPanel smart switch hardware

Reporting a problem

When contacting support, please include:

  • Your iOS version and device model
  • Steps that led to the issue
  • What you expected and what happened instead
  • A screenshot if helpful (remove private info)
