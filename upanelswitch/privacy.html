Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

uPanelSwitch is provided by an individual developer. This Privacy Policy explains how uPanelSwitch handles information when you use the app to control smart switch devices.

Information we use

uPanelSwitch does not require registration. The app stores all configuration data locally on your device, including device names, timer schedules, and room assignments. No personal data is transmitted to external servers.

Bluetooth access

uPanelSwitch uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to discover and communicate with compatible smart switch hardware. Bluetooth is used exclusively for:

  • Discovering nearby compatible switch devices
  • Establishing a connection for control commands
  • Sending on/off and scheduling instructions
  • Receiving device status confirmations

Bluetooth access is never used for location tracking, advertising, or any purpose unrelated to device control.

Data on your device

All device configuration, timer schedules, and room organization data is stored locally using on-device storage. Uninstalling uPanelSwitch removes all app data from your device. There is no cloud backup or synchronization.

No analytics or tracking

uPanelSwitch does not collect usage analytics, crash reports, device identifiers, IP addresses, or any form of behavioral data. The app operates entirely offline after the initial download from the App Store.

Third-party services

uPanelSwitch does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services. No information is shared with third parties.

Children

uPanelSwitch is a general-audience utility app and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or when legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to support@upanelswitch.com.
