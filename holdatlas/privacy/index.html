Privacy Policy

Your climbing data stays with you

Overview

HoldAtlas is a bouldering route and progress tracking tool designed with your privacy as a priority. We believe your climbing data belongs to you and only you.

HoldAtlas does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All your information remains entirely on your device.

Data Storage

All data created within HoldAtlas is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. This includes:

  • Route records (names, grades, locations, hold types, wall angles)
  • Attempt logs (results, timers, session notes)
  • Progress statistics and session history
  • Profile information and preferences

No data is uploaded to any cloud service, analytics platform, or third-party server.

Data Collection

HoldAtlas does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Photos, contacts, or other device content

Third-Party Services

HoldAtlas does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or external services. The app operates fully offline.

Data Sharing

Since no data leaves your device, there is no data sharing with any third parties. We do not sell, rent, or distribute any user information.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by removing the app from your device. Uninstalling HoldAtlas will permanently erase all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

HoldAtlas does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation or personal information input.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

For questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please visit our support page.
