Features

Crux Analysis

Pinpoint and describe the hardest section of every route. Break down complex moves and track what makes each problem challenging.

Route Logging

Record detailed route info including V-grade, wall angle, hold types, location, and quality ratings with searchable filtering.

Attempt Timer

Built-in climbing and rest timers. Log each attempt with result tracking, felt difficulty, and session notes.

Visual Progress

Charts for weekly activity, monthly volume, grade distribution, and send rates. See your growth at a glance.

Personal Profile

Track achievements, climbing streaks, session totals, and personal bests. Your climbing identity in one place.

Hold Types & Angles

Categorize routes by hold types (crimp, sloper, pinch, pocket) and wall angles (slab, vertical, overhang, roof).
