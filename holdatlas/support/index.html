Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new route?

Navigate to the Routes tab and tap the + button in the top right corner. Fill in the route details including name, grade, location, wall angle, and hold types.

How does the attempt timer work?

Go to the Attempts tab and use the built-in timer. Tap play to start timing your climb, and stop when you finish. You can also use the rest timer between attempts.

What does the Crux tab show?

The Crux tab is your dashboard showing your hardest grade sent, send rate, active projects, grade distribution, and key crux moves across all your routes.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server. If you delete the app, your data will be removed.

What grade scale does the app use?

HoldAtlas uses the V-scale (Hueco scale) from V0 to V12, which is the standard bouldering grade system.

Can I track multiple climbing locations?

Yes, each route can have its own location. Your Profile tab will show your most visited spot, and you can filter routes by location.

Getting Started Tips

  • 1

    Set up your profile

    Go to Profile and tap the edit button to add your name, home gym, and bio.

  • 2

    Log your first route

    Add a route with its grade, holds, and the crux description for the hardest move.

  • 3

    Track your attempts

    Use the timer to track climbing time and log each attempt with its result.

  • 4

    Watch your progress

    Check the Progress tab for charts showing your weekly activity and grade progression.
