Privacy Policy

iKanuLed

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how iKanuLed ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

iKanuLed is designed with privacy in mind. The app collects minimal information necessary to provide its core functionality:

  • Bluetooth Data: The app uses Bluetooth to discover and communicate with compatible LED devices. Device identifiers are stored locally on your device only.
  • Device Settings: Your lighting preferences (brightness, color temperature, schedules) are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms.
  • Usage Statistics: Power consumption data is calculated locally based on your device usage patterns. This data never leaves your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Browsing history
  • Contacts or photos
  • Any data transmitted to external servers

Data Storage

All data generated by iKanuLed is stored exclusively on your device. We do not operate any servers that collect or store user data. When you delete the app, all associated data is permanently removed from your device.

Bluetooth Permission

iKanuLed requires Bluetooth permission solely for the purpose of discovering and controlling compatible KanuLed smart LED devices. Bluetooth access is used only when you actively initiate device scanning or control operations. We do not use Bluetooth for tracking, advertising, or any purpose other than LED device communication.

Third-Party Services

iKanuLed does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Your usage of the app remains completely private.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app contains no advertising, in-app purchases targeted at children, or social features.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Technical Support page.
