Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how iDoorHudon ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our smart lock management application. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency in our data practices.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our services:

  • Bluetooth Data: Device identifiers and signal information used solely for discovering and connecting to your smart lock devices.
  • Device Information: Lock status, battery level, firmware version, and connectivity data stored locally on your device.
  • Activity Logs: Lock/unlock events, schedule data, and access history stored locally on your device.
  • App Settings: Your preferences, notification settings, and configuration choices.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Enable communication between the app and your smart lock devices
  • Display device status and activity history
  • Execute scheduled lock/unlock operations
  • Send local notifications about device events
  • Improve app performance and user experience

Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit personal data, lock credentials, or access logs to external servers. Communication between the app and your smart lock uses industry-standard AES-256 encryption to prevent unauthorized access.

Third-Party Services

iDoorHudon does not share your personal information with third parties. We do not integrate with advertising networks or analytics services that collect personal data. The app operates entirely offline after initial download.

Bluetooth Permissions

The app requires Bluetooth permission to discover and communicate with compatible smart lock devices. This permission is requested only when you initiate the device pairing process. Bluetooth is used exclusively for lock communication and is not used for tracking or advertising purposes.

Data Retention

Activity logs and device data are retained locally on your device until you choose to delete them or uninstall the application. Removing the app from your device permanently deletes all associated data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored by the application (viewable within the app)
  • Delete your data by removing devices or uninstalling the app
  • Revoke Bluetooth permissions through iOS Settings at any time
  • Disable notifications through iOS Settings

Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
