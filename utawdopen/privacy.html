Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Introduction

uTawdOpen ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our smart lock control application.

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data remains locally on your device.

Information We Collect

The App may access the following on your device:

  • Bluetooth Data: We use Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to discover and communicate with compatible smart lock devices. Bluetooth data is processed locally and never transmitted externally.
  • Device Settings: Lock configuration, schedules, and scene preferences are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms.
  • Activity Logs: Lock and unlock event logs are generated and stored locally for your reference.

How We Use Your Information

  • To establish and maintain Bluetooth connections with your smart lock
  • To execute lock/unlock commands and scheduling functions
  • To display device status, battery levels, and activity history
  • To store your preferences and custom scenes locally

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored locally on your iOS device using Apple's secure storage framework. We implement the following security measures:

  • No cloud servers or external databases are used
  • Bluetooth communication uses standard BLE security protocols
  • Data is encrypted using iOS built-in encryption when the device is locked
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. We do not share, sell, or distribute any user data to third parties.

Bluetooth Permissions

The App requires Bluetooth permission to discover and connect to smart lock devices. This permission is requested only when you navigate to the device pairing screen. You can revoke this permission at any time through iOS Settings.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information on our privacy practices. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
