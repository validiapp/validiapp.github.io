Technical Support

We're here to help you get the most out of your smart lock experience.

Getting Started

  1. Download and install uTawdOpen from the App Store on your iPhone.
  2. Open the app and grant Bluetooth permissions when prompted on the Add Device screen.
  3. Make sure your smart lock is powered on and in pairing mode.
  4. Wait for the app to discover your lock device, then tap to connect.
  5. Once connected, you can control your lock from the Home screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't the app find my smart lock?
Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone and the smart lock is in pairing mode. Try moving closer to the lock (within 10 meters). If the issue persists, restart both your phone and the smart lock.
How do I set up automatic lock schedules?
From the Home screen, tap your lock to open details. Select the "Schedule" tab, then tap the + button to create a new schedule. Choose the action (Lock/Unlock), set the time, and select which days to repeat.
What are Scenes and how do I use them?
Scenes are preset configurations for your lock. For example, "Away Mode" automatically locks your door, while "Guest Mode" keeps it unlocked. Navigate to the Scenes tab in lock details to activate or deactivate scenes.
Why does lock/unlock take a few seconds?
The app sends a secure encrypted command to your lock via Bluetooth. The brief processing time (approximately 3 seconds) ensures the command is securely transmitted and the lock mechanism completes its operation.
How do I check my lock's battery level?
The battery level is displayed on the Home screen status card and in the lock details under the Control tab. You'll receive a notification when the battery drops below 20%.
Can I remove and re-add my device?
Yes. Go to Settings > scroll down and tap "Remove Device". Then follow the initial setup process again to re-add your lock.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. All data is stored locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers. Bluetooth communication uses standard BLE security protocols, and your device data is encrypted when your iPhone is locked.

Troubleshooting

  • App crashes on launch: Try force-quitting and reopening the app. If the issue persists, reinstall from the App Store.
  • Bluetooth connection drops: Ensure no other devices are interfering. Move closer to the lock and try reconnecting.
  • Lock not responding: Check the lock's battery level. If the battery is depleted, replace it before attempting to connect.
  • Schedule not executing: Make sure the schedule is enabled (toggle is on) and the app has the necessary background permissions.

System Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 13.0 or later
  • Bluetooth 4.0 (BLE) or later
  • Compatible smart lock with BLE support
