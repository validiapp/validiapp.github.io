Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a breathing session?

Open the Breathe tab, select a mode from the horizontal list, then tap the play button. Adjust cycles before starting if needed.

Can I pause during a session?

Yes. Tap the pause button during an active session. Tap play to resume exactly where you left off.

What breathing modes are available?

6 modes: Relaxation (4-7-8), Box Breathing (4-4-4-4), Energize (6-0-2), Deep Calm (5-5-5), Focus (4-4-6), and Sleep (4-7-8).

Can I customize the pattern?

Yes. Go to Settings → Breathing Rhythm to adjust inhale, hold, exhale durations and cycle count independently.

Where is my data stored?

All data lives on your device in a local database. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. Your sessions are 100% private.

How do I export my data?

Settings → Data → Export Report. A PDF is generated locally and shared via the system share sheet (AirDrop, email, etc.).

How do I delete all data?

Settings → Data → Delete All Data. This permanently removes all sessions. Uninstalling also clears everything.

What is the Science tab?

It contains 7 evidence-based training protocols with detailed steps, benefits, and a progression path from beginner to advanced.

Does the app need internet?

No. Every feature works 100% offline. The only network action is when you share an exported PDF.

How does streak tracking work?

Complete at least one session per day. Your streak counts consecutive active days and resets if you miss one.
