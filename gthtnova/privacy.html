Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

GTHT Nova does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data externally. Everything stays on your device.

Introduction

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how GTHT Nova ("we", "our", "the app") handles information when you use our application.

Data Collection

GTHT Nova does not collect any personal data. All information generated through app usage is stored exclusively on your device's local storage.

Information Stored Locally

The following data exists only on your device:

  • Breathing session records (date, duration, mode, cycles, rating)
  • App preferences (reminder settings, sound, rhythm configuration)
  • Daily goal settings and progress tracking
  • Streak and activity calendar data

No Network Communication

The app operates entirely offline. It does not:

  • Connect to the internet for any functionality
  • Send data to third-party services or analytics providers
  • Use advertising frameworks or tracking SDKs
  • Require user account creation or authentication
  • Access contacts, photos, location, or other personal data

Data Export

You may export your session data as a PDF report. The file is generated locally and shared only through your chosen method (AirDrop, email, Messages, etc.). We have no access to exported files.

Data Deletion

Delete all stored data at any time via Settings → Data → Delete All Data. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data permanently.

Children's Privacy

The app contains no user accounts, no social features, and no data collection mechanisms of any kind.

Third-Party Services

GTHT Nova does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data. All functionality is self-contained.

Permissions

  • Notifications: For daily practice reminders (optional, user-initiated)
  • Vibration: For haptic feedback during phases (optional)

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
