Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a furniture item?

Navigate to the Archive tab and tap the "+" button in the top right corner. Fill in the details about your furniture piece including name, category, condition, location, and estimated value.

Can I set up recurring maintenance reminders?

Yes! Go to the Care tab and create a new schedule. Select the furniture item, care type, and frequency. The app will track due dates and show overdue tasks.

Is my data backed up to the cloud?

No. Oakora stores all data locally on your device for maximum privacy. We recommend regular device backups through iCloud or iTunes to prevent data loss.

How do I view maintenance spending?

The Insights tab provides charts and analytics including monthly maintenance spending, category distribution, condition overview, and value breakdowns.

Can I delete all my data?

Yes. Go to Settings and select "Clear Data". This will permanently remove all furniture records, maintenance logs, and care schedules from the app.

What iOS version is required?

Oakora requires iOS 14.0 or later. It is optimized for iPhone devices.
