Privacy Policy

Oakora

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Oakora ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Information Collection

Oakora is designed with privacy as a core principle. The App operates entirely offline and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

  • All furniture records, maintenance logs, and care schedules are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not collect personal identifiers, location data, or usage analytics.
  • No data is transmitted to third-party services or remote servers.
  • We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or similar technologies.

Data Storage

All data you enter into Oakora is stored exclusively on your device using local storage mechanisms provided by the iOS operating system. This includes:

  • Furniture collection details (names, descriptions, conditions, values)
  • Maintenance and care records
  • Care schedule configurations
  • User preferences and feedback submissions

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to any third parties. Since all data remains on your device, there is no mechanism for external data sharing within the App.

Data Security

Your data is protected by the built-in security features of your iOS device, including device encryption and app sandboxing. We recommend keeping your device software up to date for optimal security.

Data Deletion

You can delete all App data at any time through the Settings section within the App. Alternatively, uninstalling the App will remove all stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Oakora does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. The App is intended for general audiences interested in furniture maintenance.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the App and on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's data practices, please contact us through our support page.
