Getting Started

Welcome to CoolNueb! Follow these steps to set up your smart fan:

  1. Power on your fan - Ensure your CoolNueb smart fan is plugged in and powered on.
  2. Enable Bluetooth - Open your device Settings and make sure Bluetooth is turned on.
  3. Open CoolNueb app - Launch the app and tap the "+" button on the Home screen.
  4. Start scanning - The app will automatically scan for nearby devices. Wait for your fan to appear.
  5. Connect - Tap "Connect" next to your fan device. Once connected, you can control all features.

Features Guide

Power & Speed Control

Toggle your fan on/off with the power button. Adjust speed from level 1 (gentle breeze) to level 10 (maximum airflow) using the speed selector.

Oscillation

Enable vertical and/or horizontal oscillation independently. Combine both for full-room coverage.

Timer

Set automatic turn-on and turn-off times. Perfect for scheduling your fan to start before you arrive home or turn off after you fall asleep.

Usage Statistics

View your fan usage patterns over time. Track daily and weekly usage to understand your energy consumption habits.

Room Management

Organize multiple fans across different rooms. Create custom room names and manage devices independently.

Frequently Asked Questions

My fan is not appearing during scan

Make sure your fan is in pairing mode (check the fan's manual for instructions). Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone and you're within 10 meters of the fan.

The app lost connection to my fan

Try moving closer to your fan. If the issue persists, turn your fan off and on again, then reconnect from the app. Also check that Bluetooth is enabled in your device settings.

Timer is not working correctly

Ensure that your phone maintains a Bluetooth connection with the fan. Timer schedules are sent to the fan device and operate independently once set.

How do I reset my fan to factory settings?

Go to Settings > Reset All Data to remove all saved devices from the app. For the physical fan device, refer to the hardware manual for factory reset instructions.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes! CoolNueb operates entirely over Bluetooth and does not require an internet connection. All data is stored locally on your device.

Which iOS versions are supported?

CoolNueb supports iOS 13.0 and later. We recommend keeping your device updated to the latest iOS version for the best experience.
