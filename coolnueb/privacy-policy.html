Privacy Policy

CoolNueb Smart Fan Controller

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Introduction

CoolNueb ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our CoolNueb mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our services:

  • Device Data: Bluetooth device identifiers for connecting to your smart fan devices. This data is stored locally on your device only.
  • Usage Data: Fan usage statistics (operation duration, speed settings) stored locally to provide you with usage analytics.
  • Technical Data: Device type and operating system version for troubleshooting purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To establish and maintain Bluetooth connections with your fan devices
  • To display usage statistics and analytics within the app
  • To save your device configurations and preferences locally
  • To improve app performance and user experience

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, upload, or share your personal data with any third-party servers or services. Your fan usage data, device configurations, and preferences never leave your device.

Bluetooth Permissions

Our app requires Bluetooth access solely for the purpose of discovering, connecting to, and communicating with your CoolNueb smart fan devices. We do not use Bluetooth for any tracking or advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

CoolNueb does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. We do not share your data with any external parties.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your locally stored data. Since all data remains on your device, it benefits from the security features built into your iOS device, including encryption and biometric protection.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age. The app is designed for general audiences and does not require any personal information to operate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app and updating the "Effective Date" above.
