Support

Answers to common questions and guidance for getting started

Getting Started

1

Start a Session

Open the Glide tab and tap the "Quick Start" button. Select your track distance (111m, 222m, 333m, 500m, 1000m, or 1500m), add optional notes, then tap Start.

2

Record Laps

While the timer is running, tap the flag button each time you complete a lap. The app records individual lap times and cumulative split times.

3

Finish and Review

Tap "Finish" to save the session. View your lap time chart and detailed breakdown on the session detail screen.

4

Track Progress

Head to the Flow tab to see weekly and monthly volume, training consistency, and performance trends over time.

Quick Tips

⏱

Faster Laps

Enable haptic feedback in Settings for a vibration on each lap tap, so you can keep eyes on the track.

📊

Export Records

Go to Settings → Export Data to generate a PDF with all your sessions, lap times, and personal bests.

🎯

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All training data is stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database. Nothing is uploaded to any server or cloud service.

Can I switch between metric and imperial units?

Yes. Go to Settings → Units and toggle between Metric (meters, kilometres) and Imperial (yards, miles).

How do I delete a training session?

On the Glide tab, long-press any session in the recent list and confirm the deletion. To delete all data, go to Settings → Clear All Data.

Does the app use my location or camera?

No. Zenxinc does not access your location, camera, microphone, or any other device sensors beyond the vibration motor for haptic feedback.

What track distances are supported?

The app supports 111m (standard short track), 222m, 333m, 500m, 1000m, and 1500m track configurations.

Can I export my training data?

Yes. Navigate to Settings → Export Data (PDF). A comprehensive PDF report will be generated and the system share sheet will open so you can save or send it.

How do personal bests work?

After each completed session, the app checks if any lap time beats your existing record for that track distance. If so, it automatically updates your personal best.
