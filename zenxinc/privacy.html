Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Zenxinc stores all data exclusively on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Zenxinc does not collect any personal data. All information you enter — including training sessions, lap times, personal bests, and preference settings — is stored locally on your device using an embedded database.

2. Data Storage

Your data resides in a local SQLite database managed by the app. This data is never uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third-party platform. The app functions entirely offline.

  • Training session records (date, distance, laps, duration)
  • Lap time records and split times
  • Personal best records
  • App preference settings (units, theme, reminders)

3. Data Sharing

When you choose to export your data as a PDF report, the file is generated locally on your device. Sharing is handled through the operating system's native share sheet. The app itself does not transmit data to any external service during this process.

4. Third-Party Services

Zenxinc does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not contain any analytics SDKs, ad networks, or external tracking code.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All data persists on your device until you choose to delete it. You can clear all training data at any time through the Settings tab within the app. Uninstalling the app will also remove all stored data permanently.

6. Children's Privacy

Zenxinc is a general-purpose training tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age, as the app does not collect or transmit any data.

7. Network Access

The app does not require or use an internet connection for any of its core functions. The only network access that may occur is when you tap links to this website from within the app's Settings screen.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit our support page.
