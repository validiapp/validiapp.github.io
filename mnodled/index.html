Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my fan?

Navigate to the "Add Device" tab, ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your device, and wait for MnodLed to discover nearby fans. Tap "Connect" next to your fan to pair it. The process typically takes less than 10 seconds.

Why can't I find my fan?

If your fan isn't appearing, try the following:

  • Ensure your fan is powered on and in pairing mode
  • Check that Bluetooth is enabled in your iPhone Settings
  • Move closer to the fan (within 5 meters)
  • Try restarting both the fan and the app
  • Make sure no other device is currently connected to the fan
How does the timer work?

Open any connected fan's control panel and tap the "Turn On" or "Turn Off" timer cards. Set your desired time using the picker and save. The fan will automatically power on or off at the scheduled time, even if the app is in the background.

Can I control multiple fans?

Yes. MnodLed supports managing multiple smart fans simultaneously. Each fan appears as a separate card on the Home screen with fully independent controls for speed, swing, and timers.

What does the usage statistics show?

The Settings tab displays total usage hours, daily averages, and a weekly bar chart for the selected device. This helps you understand your energy consumption patterns and optimize fan usage.

What are the system requirements?
  • iOS 13.0 or later
  • iPhone with Bluetooth 4.0+ capability
  • Compatible MnodLed smart fan hardware

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Introduction

MnodLed ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our smart fan controller application.

Information We Collect

MnodLed is designed with privacy at its core. We collect minimal data necessary to provide functionality:

  • Bluetooth Data: We access Bluetooth solely to discover and communicate with MnodLed-compatible smart fan devices. Bluetooth scanning only occurs when you actively navigate to the Add Device screen.
  • Device Settings: Fan names, speed settings, timer configurations, and swing preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Usage Statistics: Usage duration and speed statistics are generated and stored locally to provide usage insights within the app.

Data Storage

All data is stored exclusively on your device using local storage (UserDefaults and CoreData). No information is transmitted to external servers, cloud services, or third parties.

Third-Party Services

MnodLed does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking frameworks, or data collection services. We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, or browsing habits.

No Account Required

MnodLed does not require registration, login, or any form of account creation. There are no stored email addresses, usernames, or passwords.

No Location Tracking

Although the app uses Bluetooth, no location data is derived from Bluetooth signals. We do not request or access location permissions.

Data Deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time through Settings > Reset All Data within the app. Uninstalling the app will also remove all locally stored information permanently.

Children's Privacy

MnodLed does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is a utility tool designed for general audiences.
