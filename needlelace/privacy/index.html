Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Overview

NeedleLace ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the NeedleLace mobile application.

NeedleLace is a fully offline application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data to external servers.

Data Collection

NeedleLace does not collect any personal information. All data you enter into the app, including:

  • Transaction records (income, expenses, material costs)
  • Commission order details (client names, project information)
  • Working hours and pricing information
  • Financial notes and categories

is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your iOS device using the system's built-in data storage mechanisms. The data persists only on your device and is included in your standard device backups (iCloud or iTunes) based on your personal backup settings.

Third-Party Services

NeedleLace does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app functions entirely offline and does not make any network requests.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any user data with third parties. Since all data remains on your device, there is no data to share.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time through the Settings menu within the app using the "Clear Data" option. Uninstalling the app will also remove all stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

NeedleLace does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. The app is designed for professional and hobbyist embroidery artisans.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
