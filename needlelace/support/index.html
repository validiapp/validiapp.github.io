Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new transaction?

Navigate to the Ledger tab and tap the "+" button at the bottom right. Select the transaction type (income, material cost, order expense, etc.), enter the amount, add a note, and select the date.

How do I create a new commission order?

Go to the Commissions tab and tap the "+" button. Fill in the client name, project title, technique, estimated hours, hourly rate, material costs, and quoted price. Set a due date and add any relevant notes.

How do I track work hours?

Open any order from the Commissions tab and tap "Log Hours". Enter the number of hours worked, and they will be added to the order's actual hours. The progress bar and profit calculations update automatically.

Can I view financial trends over time?

Yes! The Revenue tab shows a 6-month bar chart comparing income vs. expenses, along with a pie chart showing your expense breakdown by category.

Is my data backed up?

All data is stored locally on your device and will be included in your standard iOS backup (iCloud or computer backup). We recommend keeping regular backups of your device.

How do I clear all data?

Go to Settings and tap "Clear Data". You will be asked to confirm before any data is deleted. This action cannot be undone.

Does the app require internet?

No. NeedleLace works entirely offline. No internet connection is needed to use any feature of the app.

Getting Started

1. Set up your first order: Go to the Commissions tab and add your current embroidery projects with client details, pricing, and deadlines.

2. Record transactions: Use the Ledger tab to log income from completed orders and track material purchases and other expenses.

3. Monitor your finances: Check the Revenue tab regularly to see your income trends, profit margins, and financial overview.

4. Track progress: Log hours against your orders and update their status as you work through each commission.
