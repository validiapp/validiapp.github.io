Features

🔀

Merge System

Drag identical cats together to evolve them. 15 unique upgrade stages with increasing power.

🎯

Lane Combat

Enemies advance in lanes. Position your defenders strategically to cover all angles.

🏰

Your fortress wall is the last line. Repair it with coins to survive longer.

👑

Boss Waves

Every 5th wave summons a boss. Stack your strongest cats to bring them down.

🗺️

15 Levels

Progress through 5 unique battlefields with increasingly challenging waves.

🔇

No Ads. No Tracking.

Pure gameplay. No internet required. All content earned through playing.

How to Play

1

Spawn — Tap the green button to deploy a cat onto the grid.

2

Merge — Drag a cat onto another of the same level to combine them.

3

Defend — Cats auto-fire at approaching enemies. Higher levels = more damage.

4

Survive — Clear all waves to advance. Earn coins to expand your army.
