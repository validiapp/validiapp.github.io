FAQ

How do I merge cats?

Touch and drag one cat onto another cat of the same level. They combine into a higher-level cat with better stats.

How do I earn coins?

Kill enemies during waves, complete waves for bonus rewards, and win levels for a big coin payout. No in-app purchases needed.

Can I recover lost progress?

Progress is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app will erase your save data. Keep the app installed to retain progress.

The app crashes on startup

Force-close the app and relaunch. If the issue persists, restart your iPhone or update to the latest iOS version.

How do I unlock new levels?

Beat all waves in your current level. Victory automatically unlocks the next stage. There are 15 total.

Does this app need internet?

No. Feline Fortress is fully offline. Play anywhere, anytime.
