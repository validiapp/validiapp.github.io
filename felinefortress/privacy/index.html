Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

Overview

Feline Fortress ("we", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data — in short, we don't collect any.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal information whatsoever. This includes:

  • No names, emails, or identifiers
  • No device fingerprinting or analytics
  • No location data
  • No camera, microphone, or photo access
  • No account creation required
  • No third-party SDKs or trackers

Local Storage

Game progress (level completion, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

Feline Fortress does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or data collection frameworks.

Internet Usage

The app functions entirely offline. No internet connection is required or used during gameplay.

Children's Privacy

Since we collect no data of any kind, the app is inherently safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

Updates

If this policy changes, we will update this page with a new effective date.
