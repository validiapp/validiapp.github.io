Privacy Policy
Last updated: July 2026

1Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. All data you enter — including your seasoning inventory, usage logs, restock records, and personal preferences — remains exclusively on your device and is never shared with us or any third party.

2Local Storage

Your data is stored using a local SQLite database on your device. This means your information is accessible only from the device where the app is installed. No cloud synchronization, remote backup, or server-side storage is involved in any way.

3No Account Required

This app does not require registration, login, or any form of account creation. There are no usernames, passwords, email addresses, or other identifying information collected. Your identity within the app is entirely anonymous.

4No Third-Party Services

We do not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, advertising, or data collection services. No usage data, behavioral patterns, device identifiers, or any other information is shared with external parties. The app contains no SDKs that transmit data.

5Data Export

You may export your data as a PDF document at any time through the Settings screen. The exported file is generated locally on your device and shared only through the method you choose — such as AirDrop, email, or messaging apps. We never access or receive these exports.

6Data Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times. You can delete individual items or clear all data entirely from within the app's Settings screen. Once deleted, data cannot be recovered as there is no backup or cloud copy. Uninstalling the app also removes all associated data from your device.

7Network Access

This app operates entirely offline. No internet connection is required for any functionality. The app does not make any network requests, does not connect to any remote servers, and does not download or upload any data.

8Device Permissions

The app may request access to your device's file system solely for the purpose of generating and sharing PDF exports. No other device permissions — such as camera, microphone, contacts, or location — are required or requested.

9Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the app is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.
