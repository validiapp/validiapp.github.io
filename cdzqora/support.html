Frequently Asked Questions
Answers to common questions about the app.
How is my data stored?
All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite. Nothing is uploaded to any server. If you delete the app, your data is removed with it.
Can I export my collection?
Yes. Go to Settings → Export Data. A comprehensive PDF report is generated locally and you can share it through AirDrop, email, Messages, or any other sharing method on your device.
How does usage tracking work?
Open any seasoning from your Pantry, switch to the Usage tab, and tap "Log Usage." Enter the amount, recipe name, occasion, and a satisfaction rating. The stock quantity updates automatically.
What do the Trends charts show?
Trends provides several interactive visualizations: usage frequency over time, spending by month, category distribution (interactive pie chart), most-used seasonings, collection health score, and rating distribution.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. The app works entirely offline. Every feature — from adding seasonings to generating PDF reports — functions without any network access.
How do I organize by category?
When adding a seasoning, select from 14 built-in categories. In the Pantry view, use the horizontal category chips to filter. Each category has a unique icon and color theme.
Can I mark favorites?
Yes. You can toggle favorites from the detail sheet or by long-pressing a seasoning card. Favorites appear in a dedicated tab in the Archive screen.
How do I delete my data?
Go to Settings → Clear All Data. This permanently removes all seasonings, usage records, and restock history. Individual items can also be deleted from their detail view.
