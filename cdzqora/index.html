How It Works
From first jar to full pantry.
Three steps to mastering your seasoning collection.
1

Build your collection

Add each seasoning with category, quantity, unit, purchase date, expiry date, and personal rating. Choose from 14 categories with unique icons.

2

Log every use

Record which seasoning you used, how much, the recipe, occasion, and satisfaction. Stock levels update automatically in real time.

3

Restock smartly

When supplies run low, log restocks with amount, cost, and vendor. Track spending patterns and discover your purchasing habits.

Inside the App

Each tab serves a distinct purpose in managing your collection.
Tab 1

Archive

Browse favorites, low-stock alerts, expiring items. Toggle between list and grid views with search and category breakdowns.

📦
Tab 2

Moments

Chronological timeline of every usage and restock event. Smart date grouping — Today, Yesterday, and beyond.

⏱️
Tab 3

Pantry

Your main inventory. Grid or list view, search, category filters, sort options, long-press quick actions, and stock badges.

🫙
Tab 4

Trends

Interactive charts: usage over time, category pie charts, spending bars, top seasonings, health score, and rating distribution.

📊

Preferences, alert thresholds, PDF export, data management, unit defaults, currency selection, and support links.
