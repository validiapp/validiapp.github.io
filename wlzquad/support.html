Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a training session?

Navigate to the Sprint tab, select your preferred category (OLL or PLL), choose a time limit, and tap "Start Sprint". You'll get a 3-second countdown before the session begins.

How does the recall system work?

When you mark an answer as wrong during a Sprint session, it's automatically added to your Recall list. You can review these formulas later and remove them once you've mastered them by swiping left.

Can I export my progress?

Yes! Go to Settings and tap "Export Report". This generates a PDF with your complete training history, accuracy statistics, and session details. You can share it via any app on your device.

Does the app need internet?

No. Cubique works completely offline. All formulas, progress data, and features are available without an internet connection.

How do I reset my data?

Go to Settings → Reset All Data. This will permanently delete all training progress, session history, and recall items. This action cannot be undone.

What do the pattern diagrams mean?

The 3×3 grid diagrams show the top-face orientation of the last layer. Yellow cells represent correctly oriented pieces, while grey cells show pieces that need to be oriented.
