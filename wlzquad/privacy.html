Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

Last updated: August 2026

Cubique operates entirely offline. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

1. Information Collection

Cubique does not collect any personal information. All data generated through your use of the app — including training progress, session history, and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device.

2. Data Storage

All application data is stored locally on your device using the following methods:

  • Local SQLite database for training progress and session records
  • SharedPreferences for app settings and user preferences
  • Temporary file storage for PDF report generation

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export and share it.

3. Data Sharing

Cubique does not share any data with third parties. The only data that leaves your device is when you actively choose to export a PDF report using the system share sheet. This action is entirely user-initiated.

4. Third-Party Services

Cubique does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app functions completely without an internet connection.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time through the Settings screen using the "Reset All Data" option. Alternatively, uninstalling the app will remove all associated data from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Cubique does not knowingly collect information from children. The app is designed as an educational tool suitable for all ages and does not require any personal information to function.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the app's data practices, please visit our support page.
