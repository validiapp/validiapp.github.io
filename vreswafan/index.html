How It Works

Get started in minutes.

From setup to full control in three simple steps.

1

Connect Your Fan

Turn on Bluetooth, open Vreswa, and tap "Add Device." Your compatible smart fan appears automatically for pairing.

2

Customize Your Settings

Adjust speed levels, set swing modes, name your fan, and assign it to a room. Choose from preset scenes or create your own.

3

Enjoy Smart Control

Use one-tap quick actions, schedule timers, track energy usage, and manage multiple fans from anywhere in your home.
