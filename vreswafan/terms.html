2 Description of Service

Vreswa is a mobile application designed to control compatible Bluetooth-enabled smart fan devices. The App provides features including:

  • Remote fan speed control with 10-level adjustment
  • Horizontal and vertical swing mode management
  • Smart scene configuration (Sleep, Natural, Power Cool, Baby Care, Focus Work, Exercise, and custom scenes)
  • Power usage tracking and energy statistics
  • Timer scheduling and automatic shut-off
  • Multi-device management with room assignment
  • Battery level monitoring and device status alerts
