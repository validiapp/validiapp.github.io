Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

🔒

Vreswa operates entirely offline. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All your data stays on your device.

1 Information We Collect

Vreswa (referred to as "Smart Fan Control" or "the App") does not collect any personal data. The app operates locally on your device without connecting to any remote server. No registration, account, or email address is required to use the app.

2 Bluetooth Permissions

We request Bluetooth permission solely to discover and connect to your compatible smart fan devices. Bluetooth data is used exclusively for device communication and control.

  • Bluetooth is used only for fan device pairing and control commands
  • We do not track your location via Bluetooth
  • We do not use Bluetooth for any advertising or tracking purposes
  • The Bluetooth connection is only active when you are using the app

3 Local Data Storage

The app stores the following information locally on your device to provide its features:

  • Device configurations and names you assign to your fans
  • Scene settings and custom presets you create
  • Power usage statistics and runtime tracking
  • App preferences such as display and notification settings
  • Room assignments and device organization

This data is stored only on your iPhone and is never transmitted to any external server or third party.

4 No Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking services, or SDKs. Your usage of the app is completely private. We do not use services such as:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase
  • Facebook SDK or any social media tracking
  • Ad networks or advertising identifiers
  • Crash reporting services that collect personal data

5 No Network Communication

Vreswa does not require an internet connection to function. The app communicates only with your smart fan device over a local Bluetooth connection. No data is sent over the internet at any time.

6 Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your information:

  • You can delete all app data at any time through Settings > Delete All Data within the app
  • Uninstalling the app will completely remove all stored data from your device
  • Individual device records and scene configurations can be deleted from within the app

7 Children's Privacy

Vreswa does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. The app is suitable for users of all ages as it does not collect any personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided personal information through this app, please contact us.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in app update notes and on this page. The "Effective date" at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

9 Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us through the following channels:

  • Use the Feedback feature in the app's Settings menu
  • Visit our Technical Support page
