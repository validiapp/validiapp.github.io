Technical Support

Find answers to common questions and troubleshooting tips for your Vreswa smart fan.

Quick Troubleshooting

🔌

Connection Issues

Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone. Move within 10 meters of the fan. Try toggling Bluetooth off and on, then re-scan in the app.

🔋

Fan Not Responding

Check the fan's battery level. Try power-cycling the fan by turning it off and on. Re-pair the device if the issue persists.

🔄

Swing Not Working

Verify the swing mode is enabled in device settings. Some fan models may not support dual-axis swing. Check your fan model's specifications.

⏱

Timer Not Activating

Make sure the fan is currently on before setting a timer. The timer only counts down when the fan is running. Re-set the timer if needed.

? Frequently Asked Questions

What devices are compatible with Vreswa? ▼

Vreswa is compatible with Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) enabled smart fan devices from the Vreswa product line, including:

  • Vreswa SF-Pro — Premium smart fan
  • Vreswa SF-100 — Standard smart fan
  • Vreswa SF-200 — High-performance fan
  • Vreswa SF-Mini — Compact fan
  • Vreswa SF-Outdoor — Weather-resistant outdoor fan
Does the app require an internet connection? ▼

No. Vreswa operates entirely offline via Bluetooth. No internet connection, cloud account, or registration is required. All data is stored locally on your device.

How many fans can I control at once? ▼

You can add and manage multiple fans in the app. Each fan can be named, assigned to a room, and controlled independently. The "All Off" quick action lets you turn off all fans at once.

What are Smart Scenes? ▼

Smart Scenes are pre-configured profiles that set speed, swing, and timer in one tap. The app includes 6 built-in scenes: Sleep, Natural, Power Cool, Baby Care, Focus Work, and Exercise. You can also create your own custom scenes.

How does energy tracking work? ▼

The app tracks your fan's runtime and estimates power consumption based on speed level and duration. You can view daily, weekly, and monthly energy usage charts to optimize your usage and save energy.

Can I delete all my data? ▼

Yes. Go to Settings > Delete All Data to remove all stored configurations, scenes, and statistics. Uninstalling the app will also delete all data permanently. Since no data leaves your device, deletion is complete and immediate.

What iOS version is required? ▼

Vreswa requires iOS 16.0 or later. The app is optimized for iPhone and takes advantage of the latest SwiftUI features for a smooth, native experience.

My fan shows "Disconnected" — what should I do? ▼

Try these steps:

  • Make sure you're within Bluetooth range (approximately 10 meters)
  • Check that the fan is powered on and has sufficient battery
  • Toggle your iPhone's Bluetooth off and on in Settings
  • Open the app and attempt to reconnect from the device list
  • If the problem persists, remove the device and re-pair it
