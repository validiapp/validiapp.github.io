Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

iMuouLight operates entirely offline. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

Overview

This privacy policy explains how the iMuouLight application ("App") handles information. The App is a smart LED light controller that connects to Bluetooth devices and runs completely on your iPhone. There are no user accounts, no cloud services, and no analytics.

Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be explicit about what we do not do:

  • We do not collect any personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.).
  • We do not use analytics frameworks or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not transmit any data over the internet.
  • We do not store data on remote servers.
  • We do not display advertisements.
  • We do not share data with any third parties.

Bluetooth

The App uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to discover and communicate with compatible LED light devices. The Bluetooth permission is requested only when you initiate a device scan within the App. Bluetooth data is processed locally and in real time — it is never recorded, logged, or transmitted.

Locally Stored Data

The following data is stored on your device using standard iOS local storage (UserDefaults):

  • Device list — names, model identifiers, and connection details of LED devices you have added.
  • Usage records — estimated energy consumption data generated locally for tracking purposes.
  • Preferences — your chosen electricity rate, currency symbol, and display settings.
  • Notifications — in-app messages about device events (connection, timer completion, etc.).

This data never leaves your device. It is not accessible to us or any third party.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data persists on your device until you choose to remove it. You can delete all stored data at any time through Settings → Reset All Data within the App. Uninstalling the App also removes all associated data.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no personal information from any user, there is no age-specific data handling.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the Support page or contact us through the App Store listing.
