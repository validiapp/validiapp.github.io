Support

Find answers to common questions, troubleshoot issues, or get in touch with us.

How do I connect a new LED device?

Open the App, tap "Add Device" on the Home tab, and wait for the Bluetooth scan to discover nearby compatible devices. Make sure your LED device is powered on and within approximately 10 meters. When the device appears, tap "Connect" to pair it.

Why does the App ask for Bluetooth permission?

iMuouLight uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to communicate with your LED lights. iOS requires explicit user permission before any app can access Bluetooth. The permission dialog appears the first time you scan for devices, providing clear context for why Bluetooth access is needed.

My device is not appearing in the scan results.

Try the following steps: (1) Ensure your LED device is powered on. (2) Move your iPhone closer to the device, ideally within 5 meters. (3) Check that Bluetooth is enabled in your iPhone's Settings. (4) Restart the LED device by turning it off and on again. (5) Force-close the App and reopen it, then try scanning again.

How accurate are the energy cost estimates?

Energy and cost figures are approximations based on the wattage of your device and the electricity rate you configure in Settings. Actual consumption depends on many factors including voltage fluctuations, device efficiency, and real-world usage patterns. These estimates are intended for informational purposes only.

Does the App need an internet connection?

No. iMuouLight works entirely offline. All communication happens over Bluetooth between your iPhone and the LED device. All data is stored locally on your device. No internet connection is needed at any point.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings tab within the App and tap "Reset All Data." This removes all saved devices, usage records, messages, and preferences. Alternatively, uninstalling the App also removes all associated data.

What color temperatures are available?

The App supports three color temperature presets: Cool (daylight white for focus and productivity), Neutral White (balanced everyday lighting), and Warm (amber tone for relaxation and evening ambiance). Availability depends on your LED device's hardware capabilities.

How do auto-off timers work?

In the device detail screen, tap one of the timer presets (15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours). A countdown will appear showing the remaining time. When the timer reaches zero, the light turns off automatically and you receive an in-app notification. You can cancel an active timer at any time.
