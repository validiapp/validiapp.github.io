How it works

From scan to full control.

1

Scan nearby

Open the app and scan for compatible Bluetooth LED devices within range. No pairing codes needed.

2

Connect instantly

Tap to connect. Your device appears on the home screen, ready for immediate control.

3

Adjust & automate

Set brightness, choose color temperature, configure auto-off timers — everything from one screen.

4

Track usage

Monitor daily energy consumption, view weekly and monthly reports, and estimate electricity costs.

Three tones. One tap.

Switch between cool daylight, neutral white, and warm amber to match any mood or activity. Pair it with precise brightness control from 5% to 100% for the perfect atmosphere.

Energy at a glance.

See how much power each light uses, day by day. Weekly and monthly summaries show totals in kWh and estimated cost based on your electricity rate. Set your own rate and currency to get accurate local figures.

Timers that turn off for you.

Set a 15-minute, 30-minute, 1-hour, or 2-hour auto-off timer. A live countdown shows exactly when the light will switch off — and a notification confirms when it's done.

Private by design.

No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. All data — devices, usage logs, preferences — stays on your device. We never see it.
