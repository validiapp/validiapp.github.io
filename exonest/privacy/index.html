Introduction

ExoNest ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

Data Collection

ExoNest does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All information you enter into the app is stored locally on your device.

  • We do not collect personal identification information
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or other device data
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services
  • We do not serve advertisements

Local Data Storage

All data you enter (animal records, habitat items, expense entries) is stored locally on your device using standard iOS data persistence. This data is:

  • Only accessible by the ExoNest app on your device
  • Protected by your device's built-in security features
  • Removed when you uninstall the app
  • Not backed up to any third-party cloud service by us

iCloud & Device Backups

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, your app data may be included in your device backup as managed by Apple. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

ExoNest does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data processing services. We do not share your data with any third parties.

Children's Privacy

ExoNest does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not require account creation or personal information entry beyond financial records you choose to input.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
