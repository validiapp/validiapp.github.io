Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new animal to my portfolio?

Navigate to the Portfolio tab and tap the "+" button in the top-right corner. Fill in the required details including name, species, morph, purchase price, and current value.

How do I track habitat replacement cycles?

When adding a habitat item, enable the "Replacement Cycle" toggle and enter the number of days between replacements. The app will automatically track and alert you when items are overdue.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Go to Settings > Export Data to generate a shareable file containing all your records.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your app data will be included in your regular device backups managed by Apple.

How do I reset all data?

Go to Settings > Reset All Data. This action is irreversible and will delete all your records from the app.

What currencies are supported?

The app currently displays values in USD. Currency formatting follows your device's locale settings.

Troubleshooting

The app is not responding

Try force-closing the app and reopening it. If the issue persists, restart your device. If problems continue, try reinstalling the app (note: this will delete local data unless backed up).

Data appears to be missing

If you recently updated or reinstalled the app, check that your iCloud Backup was restored properly. Local data that was not backed up cannot be recovered.

System Requirements

  • iOS 14.0 or later
  • Compatible with iPhone
  • Approximately 25MB of storage space
