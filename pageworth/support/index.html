Getting Started

Pageworth is a straightforward tool for tracking your vintage book restoration finances. Here's a quick overview of the main features:

Home Tab - Asset Overview

Your dashboard showing collection value, total investment, repair profits, and monthly cash flow. The chart displays income and expenses over the past six months.

Entries Tab - Financial Records

Record all financial transactions including material purchases, repair fees, labor costs, and book sales. Filter by category to see specific types of expenses.

Archive Tab - Book Collection

Maintain detailed records of each book in your collection. Track repair progress, view valuation history, and browse your complete repair timeline.

Settings Tab - App Management

Access app preferences, provide feedback, share the app, view policies, and manage your data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server or cloud service. Your data is completely private.

How do I back up my data?

Your Pageworth data is included in your standard iOS device backup (via iCloud Backup or local iTunes/Finder backup). When you restore your device from a backup, your Pageworth data will be restored as well.

Can I recover deleted data?

Once data is cleared using the "Clear Data" option in Settings, it cannot be recovered within the app. We recommend using your device's backup feature before clearing data.

Does the app require internet access?

No, Pageworth works completely offline. Internet access is only needed if you choose to visit our support page or send feedback via email.

What iOS version is required?

Pageworth requires iOS 14.0 or later. The app is optimized for all iPhone models running supported versions.

How do I add a new book?

Navigate to the Archive tab and tap the "+" button in the top right corner. Fill in the book details including title, author, purchase price, and condition.

Can I track expenses for a specific book?

Yes! When adding a new entry in the Entries tab, you can optionally link it to a specific book from your archive. This helps you see the total cost associated with each restoration project.

Troubleshooting

App is running slowly

Try closing and reopening the app. If you have a very large number of records, the app may take a moment to load. Ensure your device has adequate storage space.

Data appears missing

If you recently updated your device or restored from a backup, allow a moment for data to fully load. If data was cleared via Settings, it cannot be recovered.

Charts not displaying correctly

Charts require at least one financial entry to display. Add a few entries and the charts will populate automatically.
