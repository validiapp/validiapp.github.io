Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Introduction

iNkuePower ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our smart remote switch application.

Information We Collect

iNkuePower is designed with privacy as a core principle. We collect minimal data necessary for the app to function:

  • Device Connection Data: Bluetooth device identifiers and names are stored locally on your device to enable pairing and control functionality.
  • Schedule Data: Timer settings and schedules you create are stored locally on your device.
  • Usage Preferences: Your app settings and scene configurations are stored locally.

Data Storage

All data is stored exclusively on your device using iOS local storage mechanisms. We do not transmit, upload, or share any of your personal data to external servers or third parties.

Bluetooth Permissions

The app requires Bluetooth access solely to:

  • Discover nearby compatible smart switch devices
  • Establish and maintain connections with your paired devices
  • Send control commands (on/off, brightness, scheduling)

Bluetooth data is processed locally and is never transmitted to any external service.

Third-Party Services

iNkuePower does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Your usage data remains entirely on your device.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties. Since all data is stored locally on your device, we have no access to it.

Data Retention

Your data persists on your device until you choose to delete it within the app or uninstall the application. Uninstalling the app removes all associated data.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is designed for general use by adults managing smart home devices.

Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to maintain the safety of your data. Bluetooth communication uses standard iOS security protocols, and all local data benefits from iOS device-level encryption.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Technical Support page.
