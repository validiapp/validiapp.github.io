Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new device?

Open the app and tap the "+" button on the Home screen. Make sure your smart switch is powered on and within Bluetooth range. The app will automatically scan and display available devices after a few seconds. Tap on your device to pair it.

My device won't connect. What should I do?

Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone (Settings > Bluetooth). Make sure the smart switch is powered on and not connected to another phone. Try restarting the switch by toggling its power, then scan again.

How do I set up a timer schedule?

From the Home screen, find your device card and tap the "Timer" button. Set your desired on/off times, select which days to repeat, and toggle the enable switch. Tap "Save Timer" to activate.

What are Scene Modes?

Scene Modes are pre-configured lighting profiles for different situations. Choose from Morning, Evening, Night, Away, or Custom to quickly set the perfect ambiance.

Why does the switch take a moment to respond?

A brief delay ensures reliable Bluetooth communication. The app confirms the command was received and executed before updating the display status.

How do I remove a paired device?

Go to Settings > Remove All Devices to clear all paired devices from the app.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes! iNkuePower uses Bluetooth Low Energy for direct communication. No internet or cloud service is needed. All features work completely offline.

What is the Bluetooth range?

Typical range is 10-15 meters (30-50 feet) in open space. Walls and obstacles may reduce range. Stay within 10 meters for best performance.

System Requirements

  • iOS 13.0 or later
  • iPhone with Bluetooth 4.0+ (BLE)
  • Compatible iNkuePower smart switch hardware
