Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new device?

Open the app and tap the "+" button on the Home screen. Make sure your smart switch is powered on and within Bluetooth range. The app will automatically scan and display available devices. Tap on your device to pair it.

My device won't connect. What should I do?

First, ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone (Settings > Bluetooth). Make sure the smart switch is powered on and not connected to another device. Try restarting the switch by toggling its power, then scan again in the app.

How do I set up a timer schedule?

From the Home screen, find your device card and tap the "Timer" button. Set your desired on/off times, select which days to repeat, and toggle the enable switch. Tap "Save Timer" to activate the schedule.

What are Scene Modes?

Scene Modes are pre-configured lighting profiles optimized for different times of day or situations. Choose from Morning (bright), Evening (warm), Night (dim), Away (security), or create your own Custom scene.

Why does the switch take 3 seconds to respond?

The brief delay ensures reliable Bluetooth communication between your phone and the smart switch. The app confirms the command was received and executed successfully before updating the status display.

How do I remove a paired device?

Go to Settings > Remove All Devices to clear all paired devices. Alternatively, you can swipe left on a device card on the Home screen to remove individual devices.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes! iNkuePower uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) for direct device communication. No internet connection or cloud service is required. All features work entirely offline.

What is the Bluetooth range?

The typical operating range is approximately 10-15 meters (30-50 feet) in open space. Walls and obstacles may reduce the effective range. For best performance, stay within 10 meters of your smart switch.

System Requirements

  • iOS 13.0 or later
  • iPhone with Bluetooth 4.0+ (BLE)
  • Compatible iNkuePower smart switch hardware
