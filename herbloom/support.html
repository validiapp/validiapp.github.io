Technical Support

Find answers to common questions or reach out to our team.

How do I add a new transaction?

Open the Ledger tab and tap the "+" button in the top right corner. Choose between income or expense, fill in the amount, title, and category, then tap Save.

Can I edit or delete a recorded entry?

In the Ledger view, swipe left on any transaction to reveal the delete option. To modify an entry, you can delete it and create a new corrected record.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. No information is sent to any server or cloud service. Your records are completely private and accessible only on your device.

How do I change the color theme?

Go to the Settings tab and tap "Color Theme." You can choose from six curated palettes: Lavender, Rosewood, Sage, Goldenrod, Dusty Blue, and Terracotta.

What happens if I clear all data?

Clearing data from the Settings menu will permanently remove all transactions and inventory items. This action cannot be undone, so please use it with caution.

How do I manage my flower inventory?

Navigate to the Bloomstock tab to view and manage your stock. Tap the "+" button to add new dried flower items with name, quantity, unit cost, and category.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes, Herbloom is designed to work completely offline. No internet connection is needed for any feature within the app.
