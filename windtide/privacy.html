Introduction

Welcome to Windtide. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information remains secure. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.

Data Collection

Windtide is designed with privacy as a core principle. We want to be transparent about what data we collect:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.
  • Local Storage Only: All your wind speed records, training sessions, routes, and settings are stored exclusively on your device.
  • No Network Communication: The app does not connect to any external servers or third-party services.
  • No Analytics: We do not use any analytics frameworks or tracking tools.

Data Storage

All data created within Windtide is stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. This includes:

  • Wind speed and gust recordings
  • Training session records
  • Route information
  • App preferences and settings

Your data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

Data Sharing

The only way data leaves your device is when you explicitly choose to export a PDF report using the share function. This is entirely under your control, and the exported file is shared only with the recipients you choose.

Third-Party Services

Windtide does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms. The app functions entirely offline.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through the Settings page within the app. Additionally, uninstalling the app will remove all stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Windtide does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not collect any personal information from any user regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date below. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please visit our support page or contact us through the App Store.
