Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new journey?

Go to the Journey tab and tap the "+" button in the top right corner. Fill in the destination, title, and notes, then tap Save.

How do I add a new contact?

Navigate to the People tab and tap the person+ icon. Enter their details including name, phone, email, and where you met them.

Is my data backed up?

All data is stored locally on your device. It is included in your device's automatic iCloud backup if enabled. We recommend keeping regular device backups.

Can I export my data?

Currently, data is stored locally. Future updates may include export capabilities. Your device backup will preserve all app data.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app will permanently remove all locally stored data including journeys, contacts, and moments. Please back up your device before uninstalling.

How do I categorize contacts?

When adding or editing a contact, you can assign them to categories like Good, VIP, or Casual. Use the filter chips on the People tab to view by category.
