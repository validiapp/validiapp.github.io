Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

iBournOpen ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our smart lock management application.

1. Information We Collect

We are designed with a privacy-first approach. The app collects only the minimum information necessary to provide its core functionality:

  • Bluetooth Data: The app uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to discover and communicate with compatible smart lock devices. This communication occurs directly between your device and the lock.
  • Device Configuration: Lock names, schedules, and scene settings are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms.
  • Activity Logs: Lock and unlock event records are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Browsing history or usage analytics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contact lists or calendar data

3. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your iOS device. We do not operate servers that receive, store, or process your personal data. If you delete the app, all associated data is permanently removed from your device.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with third parties for any purpose.

5. Bluetooth Permission

The app requires Bluetooth permission solely to discover and communicate with compatible smart lock hardware. This permission is requested when you first attempt to add a device. The app does not use Bluetooth to track your location or collect data about nearby devices beyond the scope of lock discovery.

6. Data Security

Communication between the app and your smart lock uses Bluetooth Low Energy encryption. Configuration data stored on your device is protected by iOS built-in security mechanisms, including device encryption and app sandboxing.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support page within the app or at our support page.
